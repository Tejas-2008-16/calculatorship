How to File ITR-1 Online in India: Step-by-Step for Salaried Investors

Filing your income tax return correctly avoids statutory scrutiny notices, interest penalties under Sections 234A/B/C, and delayed tax refunds. Follow this operational walkthrough on navigating the Income Tax e-Filing Portal 2.0, reconciling Form 16 with your AIS and Form 26AS, and claiming eligible deductions under FY 2026-27 tax provisions.

Key Takeaways: What You Need to Know First

  • 1. Strict ITR-1 Eligibility: ITR-1 (Sahaj) applies exclusively to resident individuals with total annual income up to ₹50 Lakh from salary, one house property, and other sources (bank interest, dividends).
  • 2. Capital Gains Disqualification: If you redeemed even one mutual fund unit or sold a single stock with capital gains or losses, you are legally disqualified from ITR-1 and must file ITR-2.
  • 3. Form 16 Pre-Fill Trap: Employer Form 16 does not capture savings account interest, FD accruals, or dividends; failing to add these will trigger automated scrutiny notices under Section 143(1)(a).
  • 4. New Regime Default: For FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28), the portal pre-selects the New Tax Regime with an automatic ₹75,000 Standard Deduction and Section 87A full rebate up to ₹7,00,000 taxable income (₹7,75,000 gross).
  • 5. Three-Way Reconciliation: Before submitting, cross-verify numbers across Form 16, AIS (Annual Information Statement), and Form 26AS to ensure every rupee of tax credit matches.
  • 6. Statutory Due Date: The filing deadline for non-audit salaried individuals is July 31st; late filing triggers fees up to ₹5,000 under Section 234F and interest under Section 234A.

The 5-Minute Pre-Fill Trap: Why Relying on Form 16 Guarantees a Tax Notice

You log in to incometax.gov.in on a Sunday in July, select ITR-1 Sahaj, click "Pre-fill Data," and see all the numbers populated from your employer's Form 16. The tax payable shows ₹0. You click submit, feel relieved that your filing took 4 minutes, and close the browser.

Three months later, an email lands from CPC Bengaluru: Intimation under Section 143(1)(a) — Prima Facie Adjustment, demanding ₹14,200 in unpaid tax and penal interest. Your employer's Form 16 only accounts for the salary they paid you. It has zero knowledge of your ₹24,000 in SBI savings account interest, ₹45,000 from HDFC fixed deposits, or ₹12,000 in stock dividends. The Income Tax Department's automated compliance engine reconciles your return against your Annual Information Statement (AIS) within seconds—and any undeclared bank interest triggers an instant demand notice.

Who Can File ITR-1 (Sahaj) vs Who Must File ITR-2

Filing the wrong ITR form invalidates your submission under Section 139(9) as a "defective return." Check your eligibility before starting:

Filing Dimension ITR-1 (Sahaj) Permitted ITR-2 Mandatory (Disqualified from ITR-1)
Total Annual Income Up to ₹50,00,000 Exceeds ₹50,00,000
Residential Status Resident & Ordinarily Resident (ROR) NRI, RNOR, or foreign resident
Mutual Fund & Stock Capital Gains Zero gains/losses (None permitted) Any realized STCG or LTCG
House Property Count One single house property Two or more house properties, or brought-forward loss
Foreign Assets / ESOPs None Holding foreign stocks (US tech RSUs/ESOPs) or foreign accounts

The Three-Way Document Reconciliation Before You File

Before entering data on the portal, download and align these three primary documents:

  1. Form 16 (Part A & Part B): Issued by your employer in June. Part A certifies total TDS deducted and deposited with the government; Part B provides your gross salary breakup, perquisites, standard deduction, and declared Chapter VI-A deductions.
  2. Annual Information Statement (AIS / TIS): Download from the "Services" tab on the e-filing portal. AIS aggregates high-value financial data reported by third parties: savings interest from all bank accounts, fixed deposit interest, stock dividend credits, mutual fund purchases/sales, and credit card payments exceeding ₹10 Lakh.
  3. Form 26AS: Your statutory tax passbook showing all TDS deducted against your PAN by employers, banks, or clients, along with advance tax and self-assessment tax challans.

Rule of thumb: If your Form 16 TDS shows ₹1,20,000, but Form 26AS only reflects ₹1,00,000, do NOT submit your return yet. Your employer must file a quarterly TDS correction (Form 24Q) to update the missing ₹20,000 credit; otherwise, CPC Bengaluru will demand that difference from you with interest.

Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Filing ITR-1 on Portal 2.0 (FY 2026-27)

Follow this exact operational sequence on incometax.gov.in:

  • Step 1 — Login & Navigation: Log in using your PAN and password. Navigate to e-File → Income Tax Returns → File Income Tax Return. Select Assessment Year 2027-28 (Current A.Y.) and filing mode as Online.
  • Step 2 — Status & Form Selection: Select your status as "Individual" and choose ITR-1. Confirm your reason for filing (e.g. taxable income exceeds basic exemption limit).
  • Step 3 — Personal Information & Regime Election: Verify your contact details and bank account validation (ensure at least one account is nominated for electronic refund). Confirm whether you are opting for the default New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) or exercising the option to switch to the Old Tax Regime.
  • Step 4 — Gross Total Income Verification: Review pre-filled salary from Form 16. Navigate to Income from Other Sources and manually add your bank savings account interest and FD interest as recorded in your AIS.
  • Step 5 — Deductions Schedule: Under the New Regime, the ₹75,000 Standard Deduction is credited automatically. If opting for the Old Regime, enter your itemized Section 80C (EPF, PPF, ELSS), Section 80D (health insurance), and HRA figures.
  • Step 6 — Taxes Paid & Verification: Cross-check total TDS against Form 26AS. If net tax payable is ₹0 or you have a refund, proceed to e-Verify immediately using Aadhaar OTP.

Frequently Asked Questions

I redeemed mutual funds with only ₹3,000 profit. Can I still file ITR-1 to save time?

No. The Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) strictly prohibits individuals with any capital gains or losses from filing ITR-1. Even if your net gain is ₹100, you must file ITR-2. Filing ITR-1 when you have realized capital gains will result in a defective return notice under Section 139(9).

What happens if I file my ITR after the July 31 deadline?

Filing between August 1 and December 31 makes your return "Belated" under Section 139(4). You face a mandatory late fee under Section 234F (₹5,000 if total income exceeds ₹5 Lakh; ₹1,000 if below ₹5 Lakh), penal interest of 1% per month under Section 234A on any unpaid tax, and permanent forfeiture of the right to carry forward capital losses.

My employer deducted tax under the New Regime. Can I file under the Old Regime?

Yes. The regime chosen on your company payroll portal is only an interim estimate for monthly TDS under Section 192. When filing your ITR online before July 31, salaried employees can choose the Old Regime, enter their 80C, 80D, and HRA deductions, and claim a direct refund of the excess TDS deducted by their employer.

Why is my income tax refund delayed after filing?

Common refund delays stem from three issues: (1) forgetting to e-verify your return within 30 days of submission, (2) your primary bank account on the portal not being "Validated" or "EVC-enabled," or (3) a mismatch between the TDS claimed in your ITR and the TDS actually deposited in Form 26AS.

Do I need to e-verify my return, or can I skip it?

E-verification is mandatory. Under CBDT rules, an unverified ITR is treated as non-existent in the eyes of the law after 30 days. You can complete verification instantly using Aadhaar OTP, Net Banking, or Demat account EVC.

How is savings bank interest taxed if I file under the New Tax Regime?

Under the New Tax Regime, Section 80TTA (which provides up to ₹10,000 tax deduction on savings bank interest) is unavailable. Every rupee of savings bank interest reported in your AIS must be declared under "Income from Other Sources" and will be taxed at your applicable slab rate.

Actionable Next Step: Download Your AIS and Form 26AS Today

Log in to incometax.gov.in right now. Click on the AIS button in the top menu bar to download your Annual Information Statement for FY 2026-27. Compare the total savings interest and dividend income in your AIS against your bank passbook. If there are discrepancies, submit feedback on the portal immediately before filing your ITR to ensure seamless, notice-free processing by CPC Bengaluru.
