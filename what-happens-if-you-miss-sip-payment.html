What Happens If You Miss a SIP Payment in India? Bank Charges & Cancellation Rules

Written by Tejas Waghmare  •  Published: 11 July 2026  •  Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

Educational guide only, not personalized investment or tax advice. Rules and rates can change; verify official sources and see our disclaimer.

Missing a mutual fund SIP installment does not attract any AMC penalty or hurt your CIBIL score, and your existing investments stay safe. However, your bank typically deducts ₹250–₹550 + GST for a failed NACH auto-debit, and 3 consecutive bounces automatically cancel the active SIP registration.

  1. 1. Zero AMC Penalty vs 3-Miss Cancellation

1. Zero Mutual Fund Penalty vs 3-Consecutive-Miss Cancellation

Asset Management Companies (AMCs) in India do not levy a late fee or penalty when a single monthly SIP auto-debit bounces. Unlike loan EMIs, mutual fund SIPs are voluntary investments—missing one month simply skips that month's unit allotment, and your existing accumulated units remain invested.

However, under standard AMFI and exchange auto-debit rules, if 3 consecutive SIP installments fail due to insufficient balance, the AMC automatically terminates the SIP instruction. To restart afterwards, you must register a fresh SIP mandate.

2. Bank NACH / ECS Return Charges (Official Fee Schedules)

While the mutual fund house charges zero penalty, your bank charges an inward NACH / ACH mandate return fee whenever a standing debit fails for insufficient funds (note that 18% GST applies extra across all banks):

BankNACH / Mandate Failure Charge (Excl. GST)Notes
State Bank of India (SBI)₹250 + GST per failed mandateStandard savings bank schedule of charges
HDFC Bank₹450 (1st return in month) / ₹500 (2nd) / ₹550 (3rd+) + GSTEscalating monthly tier per ACH return
ICICI Bank₹500 + GST per failed NACH / ECS instanceStandard retail banking schedule

Important distinction vs loans: A missed mutual fund SIP does not get reported to CIBIL or affect your credit score, because no loan repayment was missed. However, if a personal or home loan EMI bounces on the same account, credit bureaus record a late payment and both lender and bank penalties apply.

3. Better Than Letting It Bounce: Pause SIP vs Lumpsum Catch-Up

  • Use "Pause SIP" at least 7–10 days before debit: Almost all AMCs, MFCentral, and apps (Groww, Coin, Kuvera) let you pause your SIP for 1 to 6 months ahead of the next NACH presentment cycle, avoiding ₹250–₹550 + GST bank return fees while keeping the mandate intact. Compare pausing vs stopping fully in our SIP Pause vs Stop Guide.
  • Making up a missed month: Once your cash flow stabilizes, you can make a one-time top-up ("additional purchase") in the same folio to stay on track—or simply maintain your next scheduled debit and test the tiny impact of 1 skipped installment using our SIP Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does missing a SIP payment affect my CIBIL credit score?

No. A mutual fund SIP is an investment, not a debt repayment or credit line. Bounced SIP mandates do not appear on CIBIL, Experian, CRIF, or Equifax credit reports, though your bank still deducts an insufficient-funds NACH return fee.

How many consecutive missed SIPs trigger automatic SIP cancellation?

Under standard NACH and AMC rules in India, 3 consecutive failed SIP installments automatically cancel your active SIP registration (not your existing accumulated mutual fund balance).

How much do Indian banks charge if a SIP NACH debit fails?

Current public bank fee schedules show SBI charges ₹250 + GST, ICICI Bank charges ₹500 + GST, and HDFC Bank charges ₹450–₹550 + GST per insufficient-funds ACH/NACH debit return.

How can I recover from 1 missed ₹10,000 SIP mathematically?

Skipping a single ₹10,000 installment once over a 15-year ₹10,000/month SIP at 12% only changes the final corpus slightly—making a single ₹10,000 additional purchase the next month restores your original compounding path completely.
