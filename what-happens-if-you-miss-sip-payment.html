What Happens If You Miss a SIP Payment in India? (Rules & Bank Charges)

Did your SIP auto-debit bounce due to low bank balance? Take a deep breath — missing a SIP payment does NOT ruin your portfolio or hurt your CIBIL score. Here is what actually happens.

July 25, 2026 7 min read By tejascopywriter
← Back to Articles

It is the 5th of the month. Your salary was delayed by two days, or an unexpected medical bill cleared out your savings account. Suddenly, an SMS pops up from your bank: "NACH Auto Debit Failed for ₹5,000 due to Insufficient Balance." Panic sets in. Will the mutual fund company levy heavy fines? Will your existing investments be frozen? Will your CIBIL credit score plummet?

Take a deep breath. Missing a single SIP payment in India will NOT ruin your portfolio or hurt your CIBIL score. In this guide, we bust common myths, explain real bank charges, and outline the step-by-step process to handle missed SIP payments seamlessly.

1. Does the Mutual Fund AMC Penalize You? (Myth vs Reality)

Contrary to widespread belief, Asset Management Companies (AMCs) like Parag Parikh, HDFC Mutual Fund, or SBI Mutual Fund do NOT charge any penalty when a SIP auto-debit fails.

SEBI regulations treat a SIP as a voluntary investment instruction, not a loan EMI. If there is no money in your account on the scheduled date, the fund house simply fails to purchase mutual fund units for that month. Your existing accumulated units remain 100% safe and continue earning compound interest.

2. The Real Catch: Bank NACH Bounce Charges

While the mutual fund company does not fine you, your bank might. When an automated National Automated Clearing House (NACH) mandate or ECS debit fails due to low account balance, major Indian banks charge a bounce fee:

Bank Name NACH / ECS Debit Fail Charge
HDFC Bank ₹450 + GST per failed debit
ICICI Bank ₹500 + GST per failed debit
State Bank of India (SBI) ₹250 + GST per failed debit
Axis Bank ₹500 + GST per failed debit
Important Rule: To avoid paying ₹300–₹500 in useless bank penalty charges, always pause or cancel your SIP mandate at least 10 days before the scheduled debit date if you foresee a cash shortage.

3. What Happens If You Miss Multiple Consecutive SIPs?

What if you face a financial crunch for 2 or 3 months in a row? Here is what happens:

  • 1 Missed Payment: Nothing happens to your account. The next month's SIP will attempt to debit normally.
  • 2 Missed Payments: The AMC sends an email reminder warning that consecutive debits have failed.
  • 3 Consecutive Missed Payments: Most AMCs automatically terminate the SIP mandate. However, your past invested capital stays intact in your folio — it is NOT forfeited or penalized.

4. How to Use the "Pause SIP" Feature (Smart Workaround)

If Arjun (age 28) foresees a temporary cash crunch due to changing jobs, he does not need to let his SIP bounce. Almost all Indian investment platforms — Zerodha, Groww, PayTM Money, and MF Central — offer a Pause SIP feature.

  1. Log into your investment platform 7 to 10 days before your SIP date.
  2. Select your active SIP and click "Pause SIP".
  3. Select the pause duration (usually 1 to 3 months).
  4. Your SIP auto-debit will temporarily halt without incurring any bank bounce fees, and automatically resume once the pause period ends!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mutual fund company penalize you for missing a SIP?
No. AMCs do not charge any fee or fine for a missed SIP installment. However, your bank may levy NACH bounce charges for insufficient funds.
How many missed SIP payments cause auto-cancellation?
If 3 consecutive monthly SIP installments bounce due to insufficient account balance, the AMC will automatically cancel your SIP mandate. Your existing units remain safe.
Does a missed SIP payment affect your CIBIL score?
No. A SIP is a voluntary investment, not a credit card or loan EMI. Missing a SIP installment has zero impact on your CIBIL score.
