What Happens If You Miss a SIP Payment in India? (Rules & Bank Charges)

Did your SIP auto-debit bounce due to low bank balance? Take a deep breath — missing a SIP payment does NOT ruin your portfolio or hurt your CIBIL score. Here is what actually happens.

July 25, 2026 By Tejas
← Back to Articles

Picture this: It's the 5th of the month. Your salary got delayed by two days, or an unexpected medical emergency drained your main bank account. Suddenly, your phone buzzes with a notification: "NACH Auto Debit Failed for ₹5,000 due to Insufficient Balance."

Your heart instantly sinks. Will the fund house penalize you? Will your existing mutual fund units get frozen or confiscated? Is your CIBIL credit score ruined?

Take a deep breath and relax. Missing a single SIP payment in India does NOT harm your credit score, freeze your account, or forfeit your money. In this guide, let's clear up the myths, understand the real bank charges involved, and see how you can handle missed debits without losing a single rupee.

1. Does the Mutual Fund AMC Penalize You? (Myth vs Reality)

Let's bust the biggest myth right away: Asset Management Companies (AMCs) like HDFC, SBI, ICICI Prudential, or Parag Parikh do NOT charge a single paisa as penalty when your SIP auto-debit fails.

Under SEBI regulations, a SIP is a voluntary investment plan, not a loan EMI. If there isn't enough money in your savings account on your SIP date, the fund house simply skips buying units for that month. Your existing folio balance remains 100% safe, untouchable, and continues compounding undisturbed in the market.

2. The Real Catch: Bank NACH Bounce Charges

Here is where you do need to be careful. While the mutual fund company doesn't fine you, your bank very well might!

When an automated NACH (National Automated Clearing House) or ECS debit instruction fails because of insufficient funds, Indian banks treat it like a bounced cheque. Here's what major Indian banks typically charge for a failed NACH debit:

Bank Name NACH / ECS Debit Fail Charge
HDFC Bank ₹450 + GST per failed debit
ICICI Bank ₹500 + GST per failed debit
State Bank of India (SBI) ₹250 + GST per failed debit
Axis Bank ₹500 + GST per failed debit
Smart Advice: To avoid paying ₹300 to ₹500 in wasteful bank penalty fees, never let a SIP bounce blindly. If you know money will be tight this month, use your broker app's "Pause SIP" feature at least 7 to 10 days before your debit date.

3. What Happens If You Miss Multiple Consecutive SIPs?

What if you are switching jobs or facing a tight financial situation for 2 or 3 months in a row? Here is how fund houses handle consecutive missed debits:

  • 1st Missed Payment: Nothing happens to your account or folio. The fund house simply attempts the regular debit next month.
  • 2nd Missed Payment: You receive an automated SMS or email reminder from the AMC notifying you of back-to-back failed debits.
  • 3rd Consecutive Missed Payment: Most AMCs will automatically cancel the active SIP mandate so you stop incurring monthly bank bounce fees. Your past invested money stays completely safe in your account.

If you're re-aligning your cash flow and planning new investment targets, use our free SIP Goal Planner to stay on track.

4. How to Use the "Pause SIP" Feature (Smart Workaround)

If you foresee a tight cash flow month — say you're taking a career break, changing jobs, or covering big family expenses — don't let your mandate bounce. Almost every major investment app in India (Groww, Zerodha Coin, Kuvera, MF Central) offers a "Pause SIP" feature.

  1. Open your investment app 7 to 10 days before your SIP date.
  2. Navigate to your active SIPs and tap "Pause SIP".
  3. Choose your pause duration (1, 2, or 3 months).
  4. Your auto-debit halts smoothly with zero bank bounce charges, and resumes automatically when your chosen pause period ends!

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mutual fund company penalize you for missing a SIP?
No. AMCs do not charge any fee or fine for a missed SIP installment. However, your bank may levy NACH bounce charges for insufficient funds.
How many missed SIP payments cause auto-cancellation?
If 3 consecutive monthly SIP installments bounce due to insufficient account balance, the AMC will automatically cancel your SIP mandate. Your existing units remain safe.
Does a missed SIP payment affect your CIBIL score?
No. A SIP is a voluntary investment, not a credit card or loan EMI. Missing a SIP installment has zero impact on your CIBIL score.
