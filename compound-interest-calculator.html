The Compound Interest Formula Explained

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal, which also includes all the accumulated interest from previous periods. The mathematical equation is:

A = P × (1 + r / n)^(n × t)
  • A = Final maturity amount
  • P = Initial principal sum
  • r = Annual nominal interest rate (in decimal format, e.g. 10% = 0.10)
  • n = Compounding frequency per year (1 for annual, 4 for quarterly, 12 for monthly)
  • t = Number of years the capital is invested

Reference Worked Test

Investing ₹1,00,000 at 10% annual interest for 5 years compounded annually:
A = 1,00,000 × (1 + 0.10 / 1)^(1 × 5) = 1,00,000 × (1.10)^5 = ₹1,61,051.
The total interest earned is ₹61,051, an effective return of 61.05% over the full duration.
