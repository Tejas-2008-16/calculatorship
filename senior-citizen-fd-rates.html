Senior Citizen Fixed Deposits: 0.50% Premium, Form 15H & 80TTB Tax Guide

For Indian senior citizens aged 60 and above, Fixed Deposits provide predictable guaranteed cash flow. Indian banks offer an additional 0.50% to 0.75% interest rate premium, complemented by specialized tax provisions like Section 80TTB and Form 15H exemptions.

Key Takeaways: What You Need to Know First

  • Senior citizens (aged 60+) receive an additional interest rate premium of 0.50% to 0.75% across Indian banks; "Super Senior Citizens" (aged 80+) receive an additional 0.20% to 0.25% premium at select lenders.
  • Small Finance Banks (e.g., Unity, Suryoday, Equitas) offer peak senior citizen rates of 8.50%–9.00%, while Large Public & Private Banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI) offer 7.25%–7.75%.
  • DICGC Insurance: Fixed deposits across all RBI-scheduled banks (including Small Finance Banks) are strictly insured up to ₹5,00,000 per depositor per bank (principal + interest).
  • Section 80TTB Tax Shield: Senior citizens can claim a deduction of up to ₹50,000 per financial year on total bank and post office interest income under both Old and New Tax Regimes.
  • TDS Threshold: Under Section 194A, banks deduct TDS on senior citizen interest only if annual interest exceeds ₹50,000 (submit Form 15H if total estimated income is zero).

Fixed deposits are the bedrock of financial security for India's 140 million senior citizens. Recognizing the need to support retirees who depend entirely on fixed-income yields, the Reserve Bank of India permits scheduled banks to offer preferentially higher interest rates — typically 50 to 75 basis points above standard card rates.

However, navigating the wide dispersion in interest rates — from 7.25% at PSU giants to over 9.00% at Small Finance Banks — requires balancing yield against institutional risk. Here is how senior citizens should construct their fixed deposit portfolio in 2026.

Are Small Finance Banks Safe for Senior Citizens?

This is the most common concern among conservative investors. The factual regulatory reality is clear:

  • Identical Sovereign Guarantee: Small Finance Banks are scheduled commercial banks regulated under RBI's Banking Regulation Act, 1949. Every single rupee of deposits up to ₹5,00,000 (principal + interest) is 100% insured by the DICGC.
  • The ₹4.5 Lakh Rule: To capture high 9.0% yields with zero principal risk, never deposit more than ₹4,50,000 per Small Finance Bank. The remaining ₹50,000 headroom ensures that compounding interest stays completely within the ₹5 Lakh insurance limit.

Tax Rules: Maximizing Section 80TTB and Form 15H

Under Section 80TTB of the Income Tax Act, resident senior citizens can claim a tax deduction of up to ₹50,000 per financial year on aggregate interest earned from bank FDs, post office deposits, and savings accounts. This deduction is available in addition to all standard provisions.

Furthermore, if your total estimated annual income (after deductions) is below the basic tax exemption limit (₹3 Lakh under Old Regime / ₹3 Lakh under New Regime), submit Form 15H to your bank branches at the start of every financial year (April) to prevent TDS deductions under Section 194A.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a joint FD with a non-senior spouse qualify for senior citizen rates?

Yes, provided the senior citizen is the first (primary) account holder. If the non-senior spouse is named first, the bank will apply standard adult interest rates.
