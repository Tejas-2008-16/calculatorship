Senior Citizen Fixed Deposits: 0.50% Premium, Form 15H & 80TTB Tax Guide

For Indian senior citizens aged 60 and above, Fixed Deposits provide predictable guaranteed cash flow. Indian banks offer an additional 0.50% to 0.75% interest rate premium, complemented by specialized tax provisions like Section 80TTB and Form 15H exemptions.

1. Core Features of Senior Citizen FDs

Under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) directives, Indian resident individuals aged 60 years or older are entitled to preferential interest rates on domestic term deposits. Most scheduled commercial banks offer an additional 0.50% (50 basis points) over regular card rates across tenures, with select tenures offering up to 0.75% to 0.85% premiums.

2. Section 80TTB: ₹50,000 Tax-Free Interest Deduction

Under Section 80TTB of the Income Tax Act, senior citizens (aged 60+) can claim a deduction of up to ₹50,000 per financial year on interest earned from bank deposits (savings accounts, recurring deposits, and fixed deposits) and post office deposits under the Old Tax Regime.

TDS Threshold for Senior Citizens: Banks will not deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on term deposit interest paid to senior citizens unless the aggregate interest exceeds ₹50,000 in a financial year (compared to ₹40,000 for non-seniors).

3. Form 15H: Avoiding TDS Deductions Legally

If a senior citizen's estimated total taxable income for the financial year (after standard deduction and applicable rebates) results in Zero Net Tax Liability, they can submit Form 15H to their bank at the beginning of each financial year (in April). Submitting Form 15H instructs the bank not to deduct any TDS from interest payouts.

5. Special Rates for Super Senior Citizens (Aged 80+)

Several major Indian public and private sector banks offer an additional 0.20% to 0.30% rate booster (total 0.75% to 0.80% above standard card rates) for super senior citizens aged 80 years and above.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the interest rate premium for senior citizens in Indian banks?

Indian banks typically offer an additional 0.50% interest rate premium on fixed deposits for senior citizens aged 60 and above. Certain tenures and special schemes offer up to 0.75% to 0.85% higher returns.

What is the Section 80TTB interest deduction limit for senior citizens?

Section 80TTB allows senior citizens to claim a deduction of up to ₹50,000 per financial year on interest income earned from bank savings accounts, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, and post office schemes under the Old Tax Regime.

Who is eligible to submit Form 15H to prevent TDS on FD interest?

Resident individuals aged 60 years or older whose total estimated tax on income for the financial year is Nil can submit Form 15H to their bank.

Is the Senior Citizen Savings Scheme (SCSS) better than a Bank FD?

SCSS offers a sovereign-guaranteed 8.20% interest rate and Section 80C tax deduction, making it superior to standard bank FDs. However, SCSS has a ₹30 Lakh cap and fixed 5-year tenure.

Can senior citizens choose monthly interest payout on fixed deposits?

Yes. Indian banks offer non-cumulative senior citizen FDs with monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, or annual interest payout options to fund regular retirement expenses.
