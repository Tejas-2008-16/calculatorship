Editorial and Mathematical Verification Policy

Written by Tejas • Last verified: October 2026 • Applicable FY: 2026-27 (AY 2027-28)
Notice on Independence & Educational Purpose: Calculatorship is an independent, free educational website managed by a student. It is not affiliated with any bank, AMC, broking firm, insurance company, or government department. Content and calculations are created for mathematical demonstration and conceptual understanding, not as bespoke investment, legal, or tax advice. Always consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser or a practicing Chartered Accountant for personal decisions.

1. Verification Against Primary Statutory Sources

Calculatorship strictly rejects secondary reporting and unverified assertions. Every tax rate, rebate ceiling, exemption limit, and government small-savings interest rate published on our calculators and guides is sourced directly from primary statutory authorities:

  • Income Tax & Capital Gains: Sourced from Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) notifications, Finance Acts passed by the Parliament of India, and official circulars on incometaxindia.gov.in.
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF): Sourced from Ministry of Labour and Employment notifications and the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (epfindia.gov.in).
  • Small Savings Schemes (PPF, SSY, NSC, SCSS, POTD): Sourced from quarterly rate notifications issued by the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance (dea.gov.in).
  • National Pension System (NPS): Sourced from Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (pfrda.org.in).
  • Mutual Funds & Equities: Sourced from Securities and Exchange Board of India (sebi.gov.in) regulations and Association of Mutual Funds in India (amfiindia.com) industry standards.
  • Banking, Repo Rate & Monetary Data: Sourced from Reserve Bank of India (rbi.org.in) policy statements.

2. Transparent Single-Data-Store Architecture

To eliminate discrepancies between individual pages, all statutory figures (such as the 7.1% PPF rate, 8.25% EPF rate, ₹1.25 Lakh equity LTCG exemption, and New vs Old tax slabs) are tracked centrally in our structured data repository. Whenever the Government announces rate revisions, the central parameters are updated, and the site changelog documents the modification date and circular reference.

3. Income-tax Act, 2025 Transition Statement

The Income-tax Act, 2025 modernizes statutory provisions for tax years commencing from 1 April 2026. Because established judicial precedents, compliance forms (ITR-1, Form 16, Form 10E), and common terminology still widely cite the 1961 Act sections (e.g., Section 80C, 80D, 10(13A), 54F), our guides explain both the operative 1961 section references and the mapped clauses under the 2025 Act to ensure readers understand compliance under both frameworks.

4. Mathematical Testing & Precision Standards

Every calculator deployed on Calculatorship is verified using two independent calculation models before release: an algebraic closed-form formula and a discrete step-by-step table simulation. Calculators provide realistic defaults, enforce boundary checks, show their underlying mathematical formulas, and illustrate the steps with a worked example.

5. Advertising & Commercial Independence

Calculatorship participates in Google AdSense to offset server, domain, and data maintenance costs. Commercial advertising has no influence on our editorial explanations, calculations, or evaluations. We do not accept sponsored reviews, paid placements for mutual fund schemes, or affiliate bounties that bias tool outcomes. Ad units are kept strictly separated from interactive calculators to prevent accidental clicks.

6. Error Reporting & Rapid Correction

We welcome peer review. If you identify a numerical error, outdated statutory limit, or broken mathematical assumption, please consult our Corrections Page or report it directly via our Contact Form. Confirmed errors are corrected with a published changelog note.
