How to Read a Mutual Fund Factsheet: Decoding AUM, Ratios, and Holdings

Educational content only. I'm Tejas, a student. This is what I learned about monthly mutual fund factsheet disclosures mandated by SEBI, portfolio metrics, and statistical risk ratios, written with AI assistance and checked against official sources. Every AMC publishes a monthly factsheet disclosing portfolio holdings, expense structures, and statistical risk indicators. Here is an educational breakdown explaining what these metrics measure and how to interpret them neutrally.

What is a Mutual Fund Factsheet and Why Does SEBI Mandate It?

Under SEBI regulations, all registered Asset Management Companies (AMCs) in India are required to publish a detailed disclosure document called a Monthly Scheme Factsheet. This public document provides statutory transparency into a scheme's portfolio composition, expenses, risk metrics, and benchmark performance.

Factsheets are freely available on AMC websites, the AMFI portal, MF Central, and SEBI-registered investment platforms. Rather than relying on third-party marketing star ratings or short-term trailing return charts, examining a factsheet allows an investor to understand the scheme's underlying mechanics, asset allocation, and cost structure.

Section 1: Fund Basics — AUM, Fund Manager, and Expense Ratio

The introductory section of a scheme factsheet provides foundational identification data:

  • Assets Under Management (AUM): Represents the total market value of all investor capital currently held by the scheme. For large-cap schemes, deep market liquidity accommodates larger asset bases. For small-cap schemes, large AUM levels require fund managers to manage liquidity constraints and cash drag carefully according to AMFI guidelines.
  • Fund Management Tenure: Discloses the names, qualifications, and start dates of the primary and co-fund managers. Reviewing manager tenure helps investors understand whether historical performance corresponds to the current management team.
  • Total Expense Ratio (TER): Reflects the annualized percentage of scheme assets deducted daily to cover management, administrative, and custodial costs. SEBI caps maximum TER according to slab structures based on AUM. The factsheet displays both the Direct Plan TER and the Regular Plan TER, showing the commission differential paid to distributors in regular plans.
Regulatory Context: Benchmark TRI Comparison

Under SEBI circulars, all mutual fund performance metrics in factsheets must be benchmarked against Total Return Indices (TRI), which incorporate both stock price movements and gross dividend reinvestment.

Section 2: Portfolio Composition — Top Holdings and Sector Allocation

The portfolio breakdown details where scheme assets are allocated across asset classes and individual securities:

  • Top 10 Holdings: Lists the scheme's largest single-stock or bond exposures. Examining this list reveals whether the fund is concentrated (where the top 10 companies represent a large percentage of total assets) or broadly diversified.
  • Sector Allocation: Illustrates exposure to major economic sectors (e.g., Financial Services, Information Technology, Healthcare, Capital Goods). Investors can evaluate whether multiple funds in their overall portfolio have overlapping sector exposures.
  • Market Cap Allocation: Displays the proportion of the portfolio invested across large-cap (top 100 stocks by market cap), mid-cap (101st to 250th), and small-cap (251st onwards) categories, as defined by SEBI's uniform categorisation rules.

Section 3: Statistical Risk Metrics Decoded

Factsheets include statistical risk ratios calculated from 3-year trailing monthly returns compared to the risk-free rate (typically the 91-day Treasury Bill rate):

Risk Metric What It Measures Directional Meaning Mathematical Role
Sharpe Ratio Excess return per unit of total risk (Standard Deviation) Higher = Greater return per unit volatility Measures efficiency relative to risk-free assets.
Alpha (α) Excess return relative to the benchmark index on a risk-adjusted basis Positive = Exceeded benchmark expectation Indicates fund manager value-add over passive benchmark.
Beta (β) Sensitivity of fund returns to broad market index movements 1.0 = Moves in tandem with market index Beta > 1.0 indicates higher systematic volatility.
Standard Deviation Dispersion of monthly returns around the mean return Lower = More consistent historical return range Quantifies overall historical volatility.

Section 4: Portfolio Turnover Ratio (PTR)

The Portfolio Turnover Ratio (PTR) indicates the rate at which the fund's portfolio holdings are bought and sold over a 12-month period. A turnover ratio of 50% implies that half of the portfolio holdings were replaced during the year.

Higher turnover generates transaction expenses (brokerage, securities transaction tax, exchange charges) which are paid from scheme assets. Lower turnover generally reflects a longer holding horizon and lower trading activity.

5. Worked Analysis: Reviewing a Factsheet in Practice

When an investor downloads a monthly factsheet from an AMC website or MF Central, an educational inspection involves three systematic checks:

  1. Examine 3-Year and 5-Year CAGR against Benchmark: Check whether trailing annualized returns match or differ from the official TRI benchmark across varying market cycles, rather than focusing solely on 1-year performance.
  2. Verify Plan Designation and Expense Ratio: Confirm whether the statement reflects the Direct Plan or Regular Plan, reviewing the Total Expense Ratio disclosure.
  3. Audit Portfolio Overlap: Compare top 10 holdings and sector allocations across multiple schemes to assess whether combined portfolios possess concentrated exposure to single sectors or companies.

How to Think About This Yourself

Factsheet Due Diligence Framework:
  • Look beyond trailing returns: Short-term performance can be distorted by temporary sector tailwinds. Look at risk ratios (Sharpe, Beta) to evaluate volatility.
  • Check Total Expense Ratios (TER): Ensure you know whether you are invested in the Direct or Regular plan, as difference in expense ratios compounds over multi-year periods.
  • Review cash holdings: Observe the percentage of scheme assets held in cash or reverse repos; significant cash percentages in equity funds can result in cash drag during market rallies.

Where to Get Personalised Help

Official Disclosures & Regulatory Guidance:

For questions on fund selection, portfolio allocation, or regulatory disclosures:

  • AMFI Official Portal: Access monthly factsheets, scheme categorization rules, and NAV histories across all Indian AMCs on AMFI India.
  • SEBI-Registered Investment Advisers (RIA): For conflict-free portfolio reviews and asset allocation advice, consult the SEBI RIA Directory.
  • AMC Investor Relations: Official Scheme Information Documents (SID) and Key Information Memorandums (KIM) are hosted directly on each respective mutual fund's official portal.
