Corrections Policy & Public Verification Log

Maintained by Tejas • Last updated: October 2026 • Open Peer Review
Commitment to Accuracy: Financial rules, small savings rates, and tax parameters undergo periodic updates by the Ministry of Finance, CBDT, and regulators. If you spot any calculation inconsistency or outdated statutory citation, we encourage you to notify us. We publish all substantive corrections here.

How We Handle Error Submissions

When a reader flags a potential inaccuracy through our Contact Form or email, we follow a transparent three-step protocol:

  1. Primary Verification: Cross-check the flagged formula or value against the latest official gazette notification, CBDT circular, or regulator portal.
  2. Code & Content Rectification: If verified, update the central rate in data/rates.json, fix the corresponding calculator code or guide text, and re-run our validation suite.
  3. Changelog Documentation: Add a public entry below noting the file updated, the previous value, the corrected value, and the statutory citation.
