Public Accountability & Errata

Corrections Policy & Public Log

Maintained by: Tejas Waghmare • Last updated: November 2026 • Open Peer Review

1. Public Accountability When Numbers Slip

Calculator bugs waste people's time. Someone checking an aggressive home loan prepayment schedule or estimating tax outflow under the New Tax Regime expects our JavaScript formulas and statutory thresholds to line up with official circulars right down to the final rupee. Mistakes happen occasionally. Quietly scrubbing a broken formula behind closed doors wrecks trust faster than fixing it openly. Verified fixes go live quickly and land straight inside the dated table below. Nothing disappears quietly. Every change stays logged.

2. Three Correction Tiers

Reports fall cleanly into three priority buckets:

  • Type A, Computational or Statutory Fixes: Anything altering rupee results, compound rounding, tax brackets, or exemption ceilings, whether a reducing-balance EMI flaw, an outdated Section 80C cap, or an off-base Capital Gains rate, goes under priority triage until patched cleanly. Fixes ship inside 24 hours.
  • Type B, Factual or Portal Updates: Filing deadlines, updated Income Tax portal screens for AIS or TIS matching, and fresh Finance Ministry small-savings circulars get refreshed swiftly. Updates ship within 48 hours.
  • Type C, Typos & Layout Cleanup: Spelling slips, dead buttons, and mobile overflow bugs stay queued for weekly hygiene commits because formulas and rupee amounts remain unaffected. Minor edits stay clean.

3. Sending a Correction Report

Practising accountants, engineers, and careful savers spot edge cases fast. Catch a number off by a rupee? Include four quick items:

  1. Message Tejas Waghmare via direct email or drop a note on the Contact Page.
  2. Include the exact page slug.
  3. Copy the broken table figure, sentence, or test inputs showing mismatched math.
  4. Link the official circular or gazette.
