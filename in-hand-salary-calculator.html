In-Hand Salary Calculator (CTC to Take-Home Pay)

Written by Tejas Waghmare • Last updated: November 2026
Educational content only: This calculator simulates monthly net salary under FY 2026-27 income tax slabs and statutory deductions. Final take-home pay depends on employer payroll structures and individual declarations.

Direct Answer: Cash counts. Cost to Company (CTC) is the total annual expenditure your employer incurs on your employment, not what reaches your bank account. Your monthly in-hand salary equals Gross Salary minus employee EPF (12%), Professional Tax (~₹200/mo), and Income Tax TDS.

Assumes basic salary is 40% of CTC. Annual bonus excluded from regular monthly pay.

Why ₹12 LPA CTC Never Equals ₹1,00,000 In-Hand Every Month

Cash in hand matters most. Never assume gross equals take-home. Read offer letters carefully. Check pay slips. Base salary drives PF. Allowances attract tax. Know your net. While negotiating annual performance increments with human resources personnel, focusing exclusively on gross headline additions without modeling the resultant marginal tax bracket impact frequently leads employees to overestimate their upcoming monthly bank credit. When evaluating competing employment offers across Indian tech and corporate sectors, translating annual headline compensation packages into realistic net monthly bank transfers prevents uncomfortable budgeting surprises once statutory provident fund deductions and income tax withholdings take effect. Most job offers in corporate India quote an attractive Cost to Company (CTC) figure. However, what arrives in your bank account at the end of each month is substantially lower because CTC includes several mandatory institutional costs:

  • Employer Provident Fund Contribution (12% of Basic): Included in your annual CTC package, but diverted straight into your EPFO passbook.
  • Gratuity Provisions (4.81% of Basic): Companies include gratuity accrual in CTC, though you only receive it upon resigning after 5 years of continuous service.
  • Annual Variable Bonus / Performance Pay: Stated as part of your CTC but paid only once a year, conditional on company and personal KPIs.
  • Statutory Employee Deductions: Your own 12% employee PF contribution, Professional Tax (~₹200/month), and monthly TDS based on your chosen income tax regime.

2 Worked Mathematical Examples in Rupees

Example 1: Entry Level Engineer (₹6,00,000 CTC)

  • Basic Salary (40% of CTC) = ₹2,40,000/year (₹20,000/month).
  • Employer PF (12%) = ₹28,800; Gratuity accrual = ₹11,540.
  • Gross Pay = ₹5,59,660.
  • Deductions: Employee PF = ₹28,800/yr; Professional Tax = ₹2,400/yr.
  • Taxable Income = ₹5,59,660 - ₹75,000 (Std Ded) = ₹4,84,660. Under Section 87A rebate, Income Tax is ₹0.
  • Net Monthly In-Hand: ~₹44,000/month.

Example 2: Mid-Level Analyst (₹15,00,000 CTC with ₹1 Lakh Bonus)

  • Fixed CTC = ₹14,00,000. Basic Salary (40%) = ₹5,60,000/year (₹46,667/month).
  • Employer PF = ₹67,200; Gratuity = ₹26,936.
  • Gross Fixed Salary = ₹13,05,864.
  • Employee PF = ₹67,200/yr; Professional Tax = ₹2,400/yr.
  • Income Tax (New Regime after ₹75k Standard Deduction) = ~₹97,500/year (~₹8,125/month).
  • Net Monthly In-Hand: ~₹94,900/month (plus the ₹1 Lakh annual bonus post-tax).

3 Common Mistakes When Evaluating Salary Offers

Math reveals the truth. Take-home pay differs. Taxes reduce take-home. Variable pay is never guaranteed. Because state-level professional tax levies and municipal rules vary significantly between employment hubs like Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Telangana, your final monthly take-home pay can fluctuate even on identical basic compensation agreements.

1. Confusing CTC with Gross Monthly Pay

Dividing annual CTC by 12 gives an inflated figure because employer contributions and non-cash group health insurance premiums are subtracted before payroll generation.

2. Overlooking New vs Old Regime Standard Deduction

Under recent Finance Acts, salaried earners in the New Tax Regime enjoy a ₹75,000 Standard Deduction, compared to ₹50,000 in the Old Regime.

3. Forgetting Professional Tax (PT)

Most states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu) deduct up to ₹2,400 to ₹2,500 annually as Professional Tax, reducing monthly cash credit by ₹200 to ₹300.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between CTC, Gross Salary, and Net Salary?

CTC is total employer cost (including employer PF and gratuity). Gross Salary is total earnings before personal deductions. PF builds retirement savings. Look at net credits. Every deduction counts.. Net Salary (in-hand) is the final cash credited to your bank account after employee PF, tax TDS, and professional tax.

Why is Basic Salary kept at 40% to 50% of CTC?

Employers keep Basic salary at 40% to 50% to optimize statutory liabilities (EPF and Gratuity) while providing flexibility for allowances like HRA, LTA, and Special Allowance.

Is Professional Tax deductible from income tax?

Under Section 16(iii) of the Old Tax Regime, Professional Tax paid is deductible from your gross salary. In the New Tax Regime, no separate deduction is available for PT beyond the standard deduction.
