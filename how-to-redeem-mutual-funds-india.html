How to Redeem Mutual Funds: Exit Load, Timing & Tax Rules Explained

Over 90% of mutual fund content online focuses exclusively on buying: how to start your first SIP, picking top-rated flexi-cap funds, or calculating compounding projections. But very few guides address the second half of the wealth cycle: how to exit gracefully without getting stung by unexpected exit loads, capital gains tax surprises, or operational delays.

Consider Ananya, a 32-year-old marketing manager in Mumbai. After 6 years of disciplined investing, her mutual fund portfolio reached ₹25 lakh—the exact target needed for her apartment down payment. Excited, she logged into her broker app on a Tuesday evening and submitted a full redemption order. She expected the cash to hit her bank account the next morning so she could sign the builder agreement. To her dismay, the funds didn't arrive until Friday afternoon, an exit load fee of ₹4,200 was deducted because she had made a fresh SIP purchase 8 months prior, and her Tax Deducted at Source statement later revealed a hefty STCG tax liability that she hadn't accounted for!

Redeeming your mutual funds is a financial milestone, but doing it incorrectly can cost you thousands of rupees in unnecessary fees and taxes. In this comprehensive guide, we will walk through the exact step-by-step mechanics of mutual fund redemption in India, explain FIFO unit matching, break down exit load windows and 2026 tax rules, provide platform-specific guides for CAMS, KFintech, Groww, and Zerodha Coin, and arm you with a decision framework for when to exit—and when to hold steady.

1. How Redemption Works: FIFO, Cut-Off Timings & T+2 Settlement

When you click "Redeem" on your investment platform, you are instructing the Asset Management Company (AMC) to buy back a specific number of units (or a fixed rupee value) at the prevailing Net Asset Value (NAV). However, the price you receive and the time it takes for cash to arrive depend on strict SEBI operational rules.

First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Unit Allocation Rule

In a monthly Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), you purchase a fresh batch of units every month. Each batch carries its own distinct purchase date and NAV price. When you place a partial redemption order, SEBI mandates that AMCs process your units on a First-In, First-Out (FIFO) basis. This means the units you purchased earliest in your investment journey are redeemed first!

Did You Know?
Because of the FIFO rule, if you started an SIP 4 years ago and place a partial redemption today equal to 50% of your portfolio, the units being sold are the oldest ones—which are completely exempt from exit load fees and qualify for Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) treatment!

SEBI NAV Cut-Off Timings for Redemptions

The NAV price applied to your redemption depends on when your order hits the AMC servers:

  • Before 3:00 PM (Business Day): If your redemption request is registered before the official SEBI cut-off time of 3:00 PM, you receive the closing NAV calculated at the end of that same business day.
  • After 3:00 PM (or Non-Business Day): If you submit your request after 3:00 PM on a weekday, or anytime on a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday, your order is processed using the closing NAV of the next working business day.

Payout Settlement Timelines (T+1 vs T+2)

Once your order is processed, SEBI's settlement cycle governs when the money is credited directly to your registered bank account via NEFT/RTGS:

💡 Expected Bank Credit Timelines in India:
  • Equity & Hybrid Funds (T+2 Days): If you redeem an equity fund before 3:00 PM on Monday (T), the money will be credited to your bank account by Wednesday evening (T+2).
  • Debt Mutual Funds (T+1 Day): Most liquid, ultra-short, and corporate bond funds settle within 1 working business day.
  • International / Overseas Funds (T+3 to T+5 Days): Funds investing in foreign equities take longer due to global currency conversion and overseas stock exchange settlement windows.

2. Exit Loads & Capital Gains Tax: Calculating the Net Payout

Before initiating a redemption, you must calculate two friction costs that directly reduce your net bank credit: Exit Load penalties and Capital Gains Tax liabilities.

Understanding Exit Load Windows

An exit load is a fee charged by the AMC to discourage short-term trading and protect long-term investors. For most Indian equity mutual fund schemes (Flexi-Cap, Large-Cap, Mid-Cap, Small-Cap):

  • Exit within 365 days (1 Year): A 1.0% exit load is deducted from your NAV price. For example, if the current NAV is ₹100, the AMC redeems your units at an effective NAV of ₹99.
  • Exit after 365 days (1 Year): 0% exit load (Zero penalty).
  • ELSS Funds (Tax-Saving): ELSS units carry a mandatory 3-year statutory lock-in. You cannot redeem them under any condition until 3 full years have elapsed from the purchase date of each specific SIP installment.

Capital Gains Tax Rules in 2026

When you redeem mutual fund units for a profit, the gains are taxed based on your holding period under Section 112A and Section 111A of the Income Tax Act:

$$\text{Net Taxable Capital Gain} = \text{Total Redemption Payout} - \text{Total Cost of Acquisition}$$

⚠️ Pro Tip: Utilize Tax Harvesting Every Year!
Because LTCG on equity funds is completely tax-free up to ₹1.25 Lakh every financial year, smart investors redeem a portion of their profits up to ₹1.25L before March 31st and immediately reinvest it. This "steps up" your purchase cost basis, effectively eliminating capital gains tax when you eventually exit for long-term goals!

3. Step-by-Step Redemption Guide by Platform

Redeeming your mutual funds can be done either directly through the registrar or via your preferred broker app. Here are the exact steps for the four most popular platforms in India:

Method 1: Direct via CAMS & KFintech (RTA Portals)

If you hold direct-plan mutual funds across multiple apps, the Registrars and Transfer Agents (CAMS and KFintech) serve as the master gateway:

  1. Log into the myCAMS or KFinKart app/website using your registered PAN and email.
  2. Select Transact → Redeem from the main menu.
  3. Select your AMC and Folio Number.
  4. Choose between Redeem by Amount (₹) or Redeem by Units. Select "All Units" for full redemption.
  5. Verify your registered bank details and enter the OTP sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

Method 2: Via Groww App

  1. Open the Groww app and tap the Dashboard tab under Mutual Funds.
  2. Select the mutual fund scheme you wish to exit.
  3. Tap the Redeem button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Enter the redemption amount or toggle "Redeem All".
  5. Confirm your order using your T-PIN or OTP. Money will be credited to your linked primary bank account within T+2 days.

Method 3: Via Zerodha Coin (Demat Format)

  1. Log into Zerodha Coin on mobile or web.
  2. Go to your Portfolio and locate the scheme.
  3. Click the three dots options menu next to the scheme name and select Redeem.
  4. Enter the number of units to redeem and authorise the transaction via CDSL T-PIN and OTP.
  5. Payout funds will be credited to your Zerodha trading funds balance, from where you can transfer to your bank account with a single click.

4. The Decision Framework: When to Redeem vs When to Hold

Knowing how to redeem is technical; knowing when to redeem is strategic. Redeeming during the wrong market conditions can permanently destroy decades of compound growth.

GREEN LIGHT: When You SHOULD Redeem

  • Target Financial Goal Reached: You have hit your defined target date for buying a home, paying college tuition, or funding a wedding. Move the money out of equity into liquid assets 1 to 2 years before the actual event date to eliminate market crash risks!
  • Portfolio Rebalancing: Your equity allocation has ballooned from 70% to 85% due to a strong bull run. Redeem a portion of equity profits and reallocate to debt to restore your target asset mix.
  • Fundamental Deterioration of Fund: The fund manager has changed strategy, the scheme consistently underperforms its benchmark index by over 3% across 3 consecutive years, or the AMC faces governance issues.

RED LIGHT: When You Should NEVER Redeem

  • During a Market Crash (Panic Selling): The market is down 20% due to global economic headlines. Selling during a panic converts temporary paper drops into permanent real cash losses.
  • To Chase Short-Term Hot Trends: Redeeming your core diversified equity fund to buy speculative sector stocks, crypto tokens, or unverified options calls.
  • For Impulsive Consumption: Pulling out long-term retirement compounding to buy a luxury SUV or finance an international vacation.

5. The 5 Rules of Smart Mutual Fund Redemption

  1. Rule 1: Always Check the Exit Load Window. Inspect your latest CAS (Consolidated Account Statement) to verify how many units are past the 365-day exit load mark before placing your order.
  2. Rule 2: Redeem Strategy-Driven Units First. Always utilize the FIFO rule to sell older units first, ensuring you maximize your ₹1.25 Lakh annual tax-free LTCG exemption.
  3. Rule 3: De-risk Equity Portfolios Gradually via STP. If your financial goal is 2 years away, do not wait until the final month to redeem everything. Set up a Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) to move funds gradually from equity to liquid debt funds over 18 months.
  4. Rule 4: Double-Check Bank Account Details. AMCs credit redemption funds strictly to the bank account registered in your folio. If you changed your bank account recently without updating your KYC, redemption payments will bounce, causing weeks of administrative delay.
  5. Rule 5: Keep 5% Extra Buffer for Taxes. Calculate your expected STCG/LTCG tax liability in advance so you aren't surprised when filing your annual Income Tax Return (ITR-2 or ITR-3).

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel a redemption request after placing it?

If you placed your order before 3:00 PM on a business day, most platforms allow you to cancel the request online up until the 3:00 PM cut-off deadline. Once the 3:00 PM cut-off passes, the order is locked, transmitted to the AMC, and cannot be revoked.

What is the minimum redemption amount in mutual funds?

For most equity mutual funds in India, the minimum partial redemption amount is ₹500 or 50 units. If your remaining balance falls below the scheme's minimum account balance, the AMC may redeem the entire folio balance.

Does the AMC deduct TDS during mutual fund redemption for Indian resident individuals?

No. AMCs do not deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on mutual fund redemptions for resident Indian individuals. You are responsible for calculating your STCG/LTCG gains and paying applicable taxes via Advance Tax or during your annual ITR filing. (Note: TDS of 20% + surcharge applies to Non-Resident Indians / NRIs).

What happens if my registered bank account is closed when I redeem?

If the bank transfer fails due to a closed or invalid account, the AMC reverses the credit and issues a physical demand draft or banker's cheque to your registered postal address. To avoid this delay, update your bank account details via CAMS/KFintech before initiating redemption.
