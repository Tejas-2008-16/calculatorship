Mutual Fund Taxation in India 2026: LTCG, STCG & Debt Fund Rules Explained

Key Takeaways: What You Need to Know First

  • 1. Equity Fund LTCG (>12 Months): Taxed at 12.5% (hiked from 10%) on aggregate net gains exceeding ₹1,25,000 per financial year under Section 112A for FY 2026-27.
  • 2. Equity Fund STCG (≤12 Months): Taxed at a flat rate of 20% (hiked from 15%) under Section 111A, regardless of your personal tax slab.
  • 3. Debt Mutual Funds (Section 50AA): All gains from funds with ≤35% equity exposure are taxed at your income tax slab rate as short-term gains, with indexation benefits permanently removed.
  • 4. FIFO Taxation on SIPs: Redemptions are processed strictly on a First-In, First-Out basis; your oldest SIP installments unlock into the 12.5% LTCG bracket first.
  • 5. Dividend (IDCW) Taxation: Mutual fund dividend payouts are added directly to your taxable income and taxed at your marginal slab, plus 10% TDS if dividends exceed ₹5,000 in a year.
  • 6. ITR Filing Requirement: Redeeming any mutual fund units with realized capital gains disqualifies you from ITR-1; you must file ITR-2 with Schedule CG.

The Switch Penalty: How Rebalancing Between Funds Can Cost You Thousands

You have ₹10 Lakh accumulated in an active large-cap mutual fund on Groww, Zerodha Coin, or MFCentral. The fund has underperformed the index for two straight years, so you decide to switch that money into a low-cost Nifty 50 Index fund. You click "Switch" inside the app, believing it is an internal portfolio transfer.

Under Section 45 of the Income Tax Act, the tax department treats every "Switch" as two separate transactions: a complete redemption of your existing units, followed by a fresh purchase. If that ₹10 Lakh includes ₹3,00,000 of capital gain, you just triggered a taxable event. After deducting the ₹1,25,000 Section 112A exemption, you owe 12.5% plus cess on the remaining ₹1,75,000—a sudden tax liability of ₹22,750 on money that never even touched your bank account.

How FIFO Mechanics Work on Monthly SIP Withdrawals

Under Section 45(2A) of the Income Tax Act, all mutual fund redemptions are computed on a strict First-In, First-Out (FIFO) basis. This mechanism creates major tax advantages for disciplined SIP investors:

Suppose you have run an SIP of ₹10,000 per month in UTI Nifty 50 Index Fund for 24 months (October 2024 to September 2026). In October 2026, you decide to redeem ₹60,000 worth of units.

  • The AMC redemption algorithm automatically selects the units purchased in Month 1, Month 2, and Month 3 (October, November, December 2024).
  • Because those specific units have been held for over 20 months, all associated profits qualify as Long-Term Capital Gains.
  • If your cumulative LTCG from these units is ₹18,000, and your total equity LTCG across the financial year is under ₹1,25,000, your tax liability is exactly ₹0.
  • Your recent units (purchased within the last 12 months) remain untouched in the fund, preventing you from triggering the higher 20% STCG rate.

IDCW (Dividend) vs Growth: The Hidden 30% Tax Leakage

Mutual fund schemes offer two structural options: Growth Option and Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) Option. Choosing IDCW is one of the most expensive blunders an Indian salaried investor can make.

Under the Finance Act, dividends distributed by mutual funds are added directly to your gross total income and taxed at your marginal slab rate (up to 30% plus cess). Furthermore, AMCs deduct 10% TDS at source under Section 194K if your annual dividend payout exceeds ₹5,000. In contrast, in the Growth Option, the fund retains and compounds all underlying dividends without triggering any tax. You control the timing of taxation completely, paying only 12.5% LTCG upon eventual redemption.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I pay tax every month when my SIP auto-debits from my bank?

No. Purchasing mutual fund units via SIP creates zero tax liability. Tax is levied only when units are redeemed, switched to another scheme, or transferred. You pay nothing while your money remains invested and compounds.

How do I report mutual fund capital gains in my Income Tax Return?

If you realized capital gains from mutual funds during the financial year, you cannot file ITR-1. Salaried individuals must file ITR-2. Download your Consolidated Capital Gains Statement from MFCentral, CAMS, or KFintech, and enter your aggregate purchase cost and redemption values into Schedule CG.

Are Arbitrage Funds taxed like equity or debt mutual funds?

Arbitrage funds are classified as equity-oriented funds for tax purposes because they hold at least 65% gross equity exposure (hedged simultaneously with futures contracts). This means their long-term gains enjoy the 12.5% LTCG rate and ₹1.25 Lakh exemption, making them significantly more tax-efficient than liquid debt funds for high-bracket investors.

What is Securities Transaction Tax (STT) on mutual fund redemption?

When you redeem units of an equity-oriented mutual fund, an STT of 0.001% is automatically deducted by the AMC from your redemption value. Pure debt mutual funds are completely exempt from STT.

Can I set off losses from an equity mutual fund against gains in gold or real estate?

Yes. Short-Term Capital Losses (STCL) from equity funds can be set off against capital gains from any asset class (including gold, real estate, and debt funds). Long-Term Capital Losses (LTCL) from equity funds can be set off against long-term capital gains from any asset class under Section 70(3).

What happens to units of international funds purchased before April 1, 2023?

Units acquired before April 1, 2023, retain the old capital gains provisions: if held for more than 36 months, they qualify for 20% LTCG with cost inflation indexation benefits. Only units acquired on or after April 1, 2023, are subjected to Section 50AA slab-rate taxation.

Actionable Next Step: Download Your Consolidated Capital Gains Statement Today

Log in to MFCentral (mfcentral.com) or your registrar portal (mycams.com or kfinkart.com) using your PAN and registered mobile number. Download your "Consolidated Capital Gains Statement" for the current financial year. Check your net equity LTCG figure: if it is less than ₹1,25,000, you have unused tax-free room that expires on March 31st. Harvest that exemption by redeeming and immediately reinvesting eligible units in your direct funds before the financial year closes.

These figures reflect statutory provisions under Sections 111A, 112A, and 50AA of the Income Tax Act for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28); consult a registered Chartered Accountant before executing large portfolio liquidations.
