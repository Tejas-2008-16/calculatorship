3. Mathematical Simulations & Model Limitations

Calculators on Calculatorship (e.g. SIP Calculator, Step-Up SIP, Lumpsum, Goal Planner, Loan EMI, SWP, PPF, and Fixed Deposit tools) execute calculations based entirely on mathematical compounding models and user-specified inputs (such as expected rate of return, tenure, step-up percentage, or inflation rate).

  • Hypothetical Compounding: Historical returns (such as 12% or 14% on broad-market equity indices) are used solely for illustrative scenarios and do not guarantee future performance. Market-linked returns are non-linear, subject to drawdown cycles, sequence-of-returns risk, and prolonged sideways volatility.
  • Constant Rate Assumption: Most calculators assume constant annual compounded rates for mathematical modeling, whereas real-world equity returns fluctuate on a daily basis according to market conditions.
  • Tax Rate Revisions: Tax provisions (such as the 12.5% Long-Term Capital Gains tax on equities or ₹75,000 Standard Deduction under the New Tax Regime) reflect statutes passed under the Finance Act applicable for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28). Parliament and CBDT notifications may alter these provisions in subsequent legislative sessions.
