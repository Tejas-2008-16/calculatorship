Legal & Financial Disclaimer

Last reviewed: November 2026 • Independent Educational Platform

1. Educational Scope & No Regulated Advice

Tejas Waghmare built and maintains Calculatorship from Pune while studying front-end web development. Calculators, loan amortization grids, worked tax slabs, and compounding charts live here purely for practice and financial awareness. Test scenarios freely. Advice never comes with it.

Neither Calculatorship nor its student creator holds registration with SEBI as an Investment Adviser (RIA) or Research Analyst (RA), nor am I a Chartered Accountant (CA), Certified Financial Planner (CFP), lawyer, or licensed tax consultant, so every number on screen must be treated strictly as educational arithmetic rather than a buy, sell, hold, tax-filing, or legal instruction.

2. Market Risks & Return Illustrations

Mutual funds, equities, index funds, ETFs, and corporate bonds carry market risk. Read offer circulars first. Past CAGR returns never predict next decade's numbers across real-world equity drawdowns, bank deposit rate resets, or AMC expense ratio tweaks inside interactive SIP, SWP, and Lumpsum widgets that only chart steady hypothetical rates. Markets swing both ways.

3. Changing Tax Rules & Statutory Caps

Rules shift every Budget. Income tax slabs, Section 80C deductions, STCG and LTCG levies under Sections 111A and 112A, Section 87A rebate ceilings, and small-savings rates get revised regularly through Finance Acts and Ministry of Finance circulars.

Even with ongoing checks against the Income Tax Department portal and SEBI circular archive, mid-year gazette notifications change thresholds overnight, so confirm current limits straight from official notices and consult a qualified tax professional before submitting an ITR or locking capital away. Verify primary sources first.

4. Local Browser Math & Zero Data Collection

Client-side JavaScript calculates everything locally. Your numbers stay offline. Monthly salaries, home loan principals, and goal inputs never leave your device screen or reach any server database.

5. Independence & Official Portal Links

Zero paid sponsorships. Fund houses, banks, brokerages, and insurers never pay commissions, affiliate cuts, or listing fees to influence calculators here, while external portal links go straight to regulators such as the Reserve Bank of India, SEBI, PFRDA, and CBDT for direct checking.

Spotted an outdated rate? Send the exact URL through the Contact page form or write to Tejas by direct email. Corrections ship fast.
