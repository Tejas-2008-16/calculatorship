The Goods and Services Tax (GST) in India: Complete Guide

The Goods and Services Tax (GST), introduced on July 1, 2017, subsumed dozens of cascading central and state indirect taxes—including Central Excise Duty, Service Tax, State VAT, Entry Tax, and Luxury Tax—into a unified destination-based tax system under the mantra "One Nation, One Tax."

Every business registered under GST with aggregate annual turnover above ₹40 Lakh (goods) or ₹20 Lakh (services) must issue compliant tax invoices reflecting correct SAC/HSN codes and CGST/SGST/IGST tax splits.

The Core Mathematical Formulas

Invoicing in India requires two fundamental mathematical directions:

1. GST Exclusive (Add GST to Base Cost):
GST Amount = (Base Price × GST Rate) / 100
Gross Invoice Price = Base Price + GST Amount

2. GST Inclusive (Extract GST from MRP / Total):
Base Price = Gross Amount / (1 + GST Rate / 100)
GST Amount = Gross Amount - Base Price

Step-by-Step Numerical Example in INR

Suppose an Indian SaaS company sells cloud accounting software for ₹10,000 net, subject to 18% GST:

  • Step 1: Base Price = ₹10,000.
  • Step 2: Total GST = (₹10,000 × 18) / 100 = ₹1,800.
  • Step 3: Intra-State Split: If the client is in the same state (e.g., Karnataka to Karnataka), bill CGST (9%) = ₹900, SGST (9%) = ₹900.
  • Step 4: Inter-State Sale: If the client is in Maharashtra, bill IGST (18%) = ₹1,800 directly.
  • Step 5: Final Invoice Total: ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800.

Frequently Asked Questions About GST

What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?

When goods or services are supplied within the same state (Intra-State), GST is split equally between the Central Government (CGST) and State Government (SGST). When supply crosses state borders (Inter-State), Integrated GST (IGST) is collected by the Centre and apportioned to the consuming destination state.

How does Input Tax Credit (ITC) benefit businesses?

Input Tax Credit prevents the cascading "tax on tax" effect. When you buy goods or services for business operations (such as office laptops or consulting), you pay GST to your vendor. You can subtract this paid input GST from the output GST collected from your customers, remitting only the net difference to the government.

Are prices on e-commerce sites (Amazon/Flipkart) GST inclusive or exclusive?

In India, consumer retail pricing (MRP) shown to end consumers must by law be GST-inclusive. When you see an MRP of ₹1,180 for an electronic accessory, the seller has already factored in ₹1,000 base price and ₹180 GST (18%).

Invoicing Checklist for Freelancers & Small Businesses

Before generating an invoice, ensure compliance with these regulatory standards:

  1. Verify Recipient GSTIN: If the client provides a valid 15-digit GSTIN, make sure their state code matches their billing address to select CGST+SGST vs IGST correctly.
  2. Mandatory HSN/SAC Code: Every item must have its 4, 6, or 8-digit HSN/SAC code clearly stated for GSTR-1 filing accuracy.
  3. Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM): Check if the specific service falls under RCM, where the recipient must pay tax directly to the government (e.g., GTA transport or legal services).
