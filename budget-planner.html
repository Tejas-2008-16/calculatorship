What Is the 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule?

Popularized by personal finance experts, the 50/30/20 rule is an intuitive budgeting framework designed to balance living well today while securing financial independence tomorrow. It divides your post-tax monthly take-home salary into three simple categories:

The 50 / 30 / 20 Framework
Net Salary = 50% (Needs) + 30% (Wants) + 20% (Investments)
  • Needs (50%) : Rent/EMI, groceries, utilities, school fees, insurance premiums
  • Wants (30%) : Dining out, shopping, OTT subscriptions, weekend leisure, vacations
  • Investments (20%) : Mutual Fund SIPs, Emergency Funds, PPF, and retirement NPS

Why the 50/30/20 Framework Works in India

Unlike rigid spreadsheets that track every single cup of chai, the 50/30/20 rule provides broad guardrails. If your EMIs and rent consume more than 50% of your salary, the framework immediately alerts you to trim lifestyle wants to protect your 20% investment contribution.
