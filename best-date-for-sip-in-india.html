Best Date for SIP in India: Does the SIP Date Matter? (2026)

Short answer: what is the best date for SIP in India?

There is no single best date for SIP in India that reliably gives higher returns. The best date to set up a SIP is one to three days after you receive your salary and ensure that your bank account will always have enough funds and your mandate will never fail.

This is the answer. But here's why you should care. Throughout this article we'll explain why the date doesn't matter much at all (in fact, it matters less than you'd expect). We'll also show you how and why this works mathematically, and help you determine which date will be best for your cash flow. You can play around with your own figures using the SIP Calculator tool.

Does the SIP date really matter?

Almost every new investor asks Which date is best for SIP considering it as an opportunity to earn higher returns. We think that if we invest on 25th, since the market is lower on 25th as compared to 5th, we will benefit more.

The issue is that you don’t know which date will be the cheapest. The stock markets fluctuate daily. Each month and fund will have a different trend. This may mean that one month the 5th is cheaper but the next month the 25th is cheaper. However, over a long SIP (10, 15 or 20 years) the small fluctuations will cancel each other out. Multiple Indian investing websites have looked into comparing SIP dates over long periods and they all state that the difference between the best and worst date is so small that it wouldn’t be worth basing an investment plan on.

The same principle applies here as Rupee Cost Averaging. The SIP helps as you are investing regularly regardless of market conditions.

How does the SIP date work?

Before comparing dates, It helps to understand what happens when you perform a SIP.

  • You provide a mandate. When you perform SIP, you give your bank permission to pay a fixed amount on your selected date every month using an e-mandate like NACH/UPI AutoPay.
  • Amount is debited and units allocated. The amount will be used to purchase mutual fund units based on the Net Asset Value of that installment as per the scheme.
  • Holidays affect date. If your SIP falls on a bank holiday or a market closure day, then it will usually be processed on the next working day.
  • Limited dates available. Most AMCs offer several dates ranging from the 1st to the 28th. Some funds will have more so it's best to look into your AMC or platform to see what dates you can choose.

Example with calculation (Assuming SIP is done on 5th and 25th)

To illustrate, let us try with some made up figures. The NAVs below are purely illustrative. They do not reflect actual fund data and should not be taken as a forecast.
Assume a SIP of ₹10,000 per month for six months. In this imaginary fund, the NAV on the 5th and on the 25th of each month is as follows.

Month NAV on 5th Units bought on 5th NAV on 25th Units bought on 25th
Month 1 ₹100 100.000 ₹97 103.093
Month 2 ₹98 102.041 ₹96 104.167
Month 3 ₹95 105.263 ₹97 103.093
Month 4 ₹99 101.010 ₹101 99.010
Month 5 ₹103 97.087 ₹105 95.238
Month 6 ₹106 94.340 ₹104 96.154
Total units 599.741 600.754

Total money invested is ₹60,000 in both cases. Suppose the NAV is ₹110 at the end of month six.

  • Value of the SIP on the 5th: 599.741 units × ₹110 = about ₹65,972
  • Value of the SIP on the 25th: 600.754 units × ₹110 = about ₹66,083

In this hypothetical example, 25th outperforms by around ₹111 (0.17%). This is negligible. If we reverse the trend of the NAV values, such that the 5th is the cheaper date more often, then the 5th would have outperformed by a similar amount. This is why no date will consistently perform better in the long term.

The key point to note here is that the final value has only changed by a fraction of a percent due to the date. Everything else has remained the same.

What really affects your SIP amount?

If the date doesn’t matter so much then what really does? There are 3 factors that will have a far greater impact on your SIP amount. All of these can be controlled.

1. Return on Investment: A SIP of ₹10,000 per month invested for 20 years at a return of 12% per annum will give you a corpus of approximately ₹99.9 lakh If you instead get a return of 11%, your corpus will be around ₹87.4 lakh. This is a huge difference of around ₹12.5 lakh, due to a mere 1% change in your return. This shows how important it is to keep costs down. There could be a cost difference between Regular and Direct plans of 0.5% to 1.5% per annum. You can find out more here Direct vs Regular Mutual Funds.

2. You will never miss out on paying into your SIP. If your SIP is bouncing due to insufficient funds then you are not purchasing units. Choosing the date to fit with when you receive your salary will help you avoid this much more than trying to time the market.

3. Gradually increasing your SIP. Increasing your SIP annually by a fixed amount (in line with the increase in your salary) will have a much greater impact. You can use our Step-Up SIP Calculator to model this.

This is just an example of what sort of numbers can be input into the equation. In reality, you will get different results and there is always a risk of losing out in the stock market when investing in equity mutual funds.

Which date should I choose for my SIP?

Rather than thinking about what the best date is based on the numbers, think about what is the safest option for you. The table below is a general guide only.

SIP Date Who should do this? Notes
1st-3rd Those whose salary gets credited into their account on the last working day of the previous month. If your salary arrives late, the mandate can fail. Many other payments such as EMIs and bills also cluster at the start of the month
5th to 7th Most salaried employees who are paid between the 1st and the 3rd A good default. Salary has usually settled and the first rush of payments is over
10th People paid on the 5th to 7th Leaves a small buffer after payday
15th People who get paid mid-month, or who receive two payments a month Check that the date works with your other mandates
25th to 28th Freelancers, business owners or anyone with irregular income who wants to invest from what is left at month-end Easy to skip or postpone if you are short of cash. Dates after the 28th are not always available

If you have variable earnings then have a small cash reserve within the account that will fund your SIP. Choose to invest on a date just after your most reliable payment date.

Myths regarding the best date to do your SIP

Myth 1:The last Thursday of the month is best – This is based on the assumption that there is higher market volatility at the end of the month due to derivatives expiries. This means that you can get more units for less. There is no actual reliable evidence that using this date will provide better returns in the long term. Additionally the derivatives expiry date can be changed by exchanges.

Myth 2: Investing on the 1st. Many people believe that investing on the 1st will give your money more time to grow, however the number of days is very insignificant when compared to the period of time you are investing for. This could be across 12 months, 5 years or 20 years.

Myth 3: "If I can find the best date to invest I will beat the market." SIPs are designed for those who don't want to time the market. Searching for 'the best date' is the opposite of what automated regular investing is all about.

5 Simple Steps to Decide SIP Date

  1. Know your salary credit date. Observe the last three months and consider the last date when your salary got credited.
  2. Consider a 2-3 day cushion. For example – Your salary is credited on the 1st so you can consider 4th/5th as your SIP date.
  3. Consider the other automatic deductions from your account Don’t consider all your EMIs, rent, insurance and credit card payments on the same date.
  4. Know the dates offered by your AMC. Choose a date which will be available for your scheme.
  5. Be consistent Once you decide on a date, ensure that you stay consistent. Changing date every month will make things cumbersome and confusing, but will not add any returns.

Should I split my SIP?

Some investors like to split their monthly SIP into two parts, for example paying half on the 5th and half on the 20th. This doesn’t really affect the amount you will save over time but can be useful if you get paid twice a month and want to feel less vulnerable on one day. You will have more mandates to track. Unless there is a specific reason for doing so it makes things easier with just one date.

Common mistakes when choosing an SIP date

  • Date selection before salary is the most common reason for failed installments.
  • All the auto-debits to occur on one day. This can lead to multiple installments failing.
  • Not considering bounce policies. There could be charges by the bank for failed debits. Many AMCS may stop your SIP if they have a few failed installments. Make sure you are aware of your schemes and AMC policies.
  • Stopping your SIP during market downturns. This will usually have more negative impact than date selection. You can see the math behind this here SIP Pause vs Stop vs Continue.
  • Changing dates often in an attempt to increase returns. This will cost you extra and cause confusion with no real benefit.

Does SIP Date affect taxation?

Your SIP date does not affect your tax rate, however it does determine how long you will have held each installment. Under Indian tax regulations all installments of your SIP will be considered individual purchases. When redeeming your units, they will be redeemed using the First In First Out (FIFO) rule. Any gains made on equity oriented funds will be considered long term if you have held the unit for over 12 months. As per the tax rules for FY 2026-27 any long term gains exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh will be taxed at 12.5% and short term gains will be taxed at 20%. The tax rules can change so please always check with the Income Tax Department or a Qualified Chartered Accountant for the latest rates. We have a detailed guide on Capital Gains Tax available here.

Some quick checks to do before you decide your SIP date:

  • The SIP date is a little after my salary is credited into my account.
  • I have some flexibility in my bank account.
  • The EMI's and other automatic payments are on different days.
  • This date is available on my AMC for the scheme I'm investing in.
  • I know this won't make a difference to my returns.
  • I'll increase my SIP when I have more income.

How to think about this yourself ?

Ask yourself the following questions:

  • Will the money be available on this date every month (including during a busy month?) ?
  • Are you selecting this date purely because it suits you or because you believe it will outperform?
  • Have you considered fund costs (expense ratios) and are you investing in a Direct plan?
  • Is your SIP amount a comfortable amount to invest if your income falls for a short period of time?

FAQs

What would be the best date for SIP?

There is no date that has been proven to provide better returns in the long run. However, for salaried folks, choosing a couple of days after your salary gets credited can be a good option. Choosing a date between 5th to 7th minimizes the chances of SIP failure.

Is it better to take the 1st or the 5th for SIP?

Both are equally good for returns. However, the 5th is often a better option for salaried individuals as you will have already received your salary and your payments at the beginning of the month will have been processed. If your salary is paid before the 1st, then the 1st could be a good option too.

Any good investing in SIP on last date?

The dates from 25th till 28th are suitable for people like freelancers who would like to invest leftover money at the end of the month. There is no benefit in terms of returns. Also, some funds may not have option beyond 28th.

If my SIP date falls on a holiday?

Generally, the amount will be invested on the next working day. Please refer to your AMC terms

If my SIP fails due to insufficient funds?

No amount will be invested for that month. Your bank may also charge a fee. Many AMCs may also stop your SIP after multiple failures. Please leave some buffer and refer scheme terms.

Can I change my SIP date?

Most AMCs and platforms allow you to change the SIP date or start a new SIP and cancel the previous one. Units purchased will not be affected by any changes.

Should I set different SIP dates for different funds?

Yes you can, however there is no advantage in doing so. You would only do this if you are receiving money on different dates or want to reduce the number of debits you are making.

Does changing my SIP date affect taxation?

No. It does not affect any taxation. It simply dictates when the holding period starts for each installment as each installment is considered as a separate purchase.

Final Thoughts

In the search to find the best SIP date in India, it is ultimately a search for certainty which you can't get from the market. To simplify, just pick a logical date a few days after you receive your salary, ensure you're not getting any bad installments, keep the costs down and increase your SIP as your income increases. Set a date, set a mandate and let the magic of compounding work.
