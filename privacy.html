Privacy Policy for Calculatorship

Last updated: September 2026

At Calculatorship (accessible from https://www.calculatorship.in), one of our primary commitments is the total transparency of our technical operations and the protection of our visitors' digital privacy. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information that are collected, recorded, and processed when you visit our website, how third-party advertising and analytics services operate on our platform, and your legal rights under applicable privacy frameworks including the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 of India.

If you have additional questions or require further information regarding our privacy practices, please do not hesitate to contact our editorial desk at contact@calculatorship.in.

1. Client-Side Calculation Privacy (Zero Financial Data Logging)

Calculatorship is architected under a strict Privacy-by-Design principle. Unlike traditional financial services portals, our investment calculators (including SIP, Step-Up SIP, Lumpsum, Loan EMI, Fixed Deposit, PPF, SWP, Budget Planner, and Income Tax calculators) run 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

  • We never ask for, record, transmit, or store your name, phone number, Permanent Account Number (PAN), Aadhaar, bank details, monthly income, or investment balances.
  • All computational inputs you adjust via sliders or input boxes are processed in your device's local memory and are completely cleared when you refresh or close your browser tab.
  • We do not create personal financial profiles, credit risk scores, or investor classifications.

2. Information We Automatically Collect

Like almost all modern educational web platforms, Calculatorship collects standard technical log data when you navigate our pages. This information includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) Addresses: Used in an aggregated, anonymized format to monitor server health, diagnose technical routing issues, and prevent automated distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks.
  • Browser Type and Device Specifications: Information such as whether you are using Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge, and screen resolution, which helps us ensure our calculator layouts render cleanly across smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) and Time Stamps: Standard server access log metrics indicating date and time of visits.
  • Referring and Exit Pages: Aggregated data regarding how visitors navigate through our guides, calculators, and educational articles.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

Calculatorship uses Google AdSense to display advertisements across educational pages to fund our free hosting, independent research, and continuous software maintenance. In compliance with Google AdSense Publisher Policies, we provide the following explicit disclosure:

"Calculatorship uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies and web beacons to show ads based on users' prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads. Third-party advertising partners may use cookies and other tracking technologies to collect information about users' activities on this and other websites to provide targeted advertising based on interests. For more information about this practice and to know your choices about not having this information used by these companies, visit http://www.aboutads.info/choices/."

Google and its certified third-party vendor networks may use cookies to serve personalized advertisements based on your prior browsing history. You have full granular control over whether personalized cookies are permitted through our on-site cookie consent manager as well as your individual web browser settings.

4. Google Analytics 4 (GA4)

We utilize Google Analytics 4 (GA4), a web analytics service provided by Google LLC, to understand how visitors engage with our tools and articles (such as average page duration, most popular calculators, and bounce rates). GA4 utilizes cookies to record non-personally identifiable telemetry data.

We have configured Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled. The aggregated data is transmitted to and stored by Google on secure global servers. Google uses this information on our behalf to compile analytical reports on platform performance. You can review Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on at https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

5. Google Search Console

Calculatorship utilizes Google Search Console for domain verification, XML sitemap indexing, crawl error diagnostic reviews, Core Web Vitals monitoring, and organic search indexation analytics. Google Search Console does not collect personal user data or track individual user identities on our website.

6. Cookie Policy & Management

A cookie is a small text file saved to your device by your web browser. Calculatorship uses cookies for the following specific purposes:

  • Essential & Functional Cookies: Store your UI preferences (such as light or dark theme mode) and preserve your cookie consent selection across browsing sessions. These do not contain personal information and persist for up to 12 months.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Placed by Google Analytics 4 to measure aggregate usage patterns.
  • Advertising Cookies: Placed by Google AdSense upon explicit user consent to serve relevant, non-intrusive advertisements.

You can adjust your cookie settings at any time by clearing your browser cache and cookies, or configuring your browser to block third-party cookies by default.

7. External Links & Third-Party Websites

Our educational guides and calculator references contain hyperlinks to external regulatory authorities and authoritative institutions, including the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI), Reserve Bank of India (RBI), Ministry of Finance, and Income Tax Department. Once you leave our domain via an external link, this Privacy Policy no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

8. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & Indian DPDP Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of young individuals online is crucial. Calculatorship is designed for adults, salaried earners, and adult retail investors seeking personal finance tools. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 18 under Indian law). If a parent or guardian believes that a minor has provided information through our contact form, please notify us immediately at contact@calculatorship.in, and we will promptly delete such records.

9. User Rights Under India's DPDP Act, 2023

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and international data privacy benchmarks, users accessing Calculatorship are entitled to:

  • Right to Information: The right to know whether any personal data is being processed (as noted above, our calculators collect zero personal financial data).
  • Right to Correction and Erasure: The right to request the deletion or correction of any correspondence records if you have emailed us via our contact form.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: The right to lodge inquiries or complaints regarding data protection by emailing our Data Grievance Officer at contact@calculatorship.in.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in statutory laws, regulatory directives from SEBI/RBI, or modifications to Google publisher policies. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
