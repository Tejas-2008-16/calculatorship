Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Calculators Run Locally in Your Browser

Every financial calculator on Calculatorship executes locally inside your browser tab using client-side JavaScript. Salary figures, SIP contributions, loan amounts, and tax deductions you enter are processed in your device memory and are never transmitted to or stored in a Calculatorship backend database.

2. Analytics & Performance Measurement

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to review aggregate traffic metrics such as page views, screen sizes, and page loading speed so we can fix mobile layout bugs and prioritize new calculators. Your individual calculator inputs are never captured by analytics. You can opt out using browser cookie controls or the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosure

Calculatorship displays advertising through Google AdSense to help cover hosting and domain costs. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this site or other websites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or via aboutads.info. Learn more in Google's Advertising Privacy Policy. Calculatorship never sells, rents, or shares phone numbers or financial details with loan brokers or telemarketers.

4. Contact Messages & Feedback

If you contact us via our Contact page or email contact@calculatorship.in to report a rounding issue or outdated tax limit, your message is used solely to review and reply to your inquiry and is never added to third-party marketing lists.

5. Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Calculatorship publishes general financial literacy guides and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. Any material changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
