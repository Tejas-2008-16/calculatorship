Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Calculators Run in Your Browser

All 21 tools run directly inside your local tab using plain JavaScript. Nothing leaves your device. Entered salary figures, SIP inputs, tax deductions, and loan numbers never get saved onto remote databases for account profiling anywhere.

2. Analytics & Cookies

Google Analytics 4 logs aggregate visitor counts, screen sizes, and slow-loading routes so broken calculators or mobile table overflows get repaired quickly. Entered numbers stay uncollected. Analytics cookies can be disabled anytime inside browser preferences.

3. Separated Display Ads, Zero Spam

Clearly marked Google AdSense boxes sit separately from editorial guides instead of fake bank comparison popups or loan callback traps. Lead brokers stay banned. Nobody here sells, rents, or trades user records with insurance sellers.

4. Contact & Feedback Submissions

Notes submitted through the contact page or sent to email are checked solely to debug calculation rounding errors, patch changed statutory tax limits, or evaluate topic requests without pushing your address onto external mailing lists.

5. Children's Privacy

Calculatorship shares general financial literacy articles and math utilities without intentionally gathering personal details from minors under 18.

6. Changes to This Policy

Whenever privacy disclosures change after browser or tool updates, the revision stamp at the top reflects that date cleanly. Check periodically anytime.
