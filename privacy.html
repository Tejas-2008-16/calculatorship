Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

Calculatorship ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website at calculatorship.in. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of our financial calculators and related content.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

All calculator inputs — including monthly investment amounts, expected return rates, investment durations, and target wealth goals — are processed entirely within your web browser using client-side JavaScript. We do not transmit, store, or process any of your financial inputs on our servers. We have no visibility into any numbers you enter into our tools.

2. Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we may collect certain standard technical information through our hosting infrastructure and analytics services. This includes IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and pages visited. This data is used in aggregate form only to understand traffic patterns and improve site performance.

3. Cookies

We store your preferred display theme (dark or light mode) in your browser's localStorage. This preference never leaves your device. For full details about our cookie usage, please read our Cookie Policy.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We participate in the Google AdSense program to display contextually relevant advertisements. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history and interests. Review Google's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Contact & Editorial Desk

When you submit our contact form or write to author Tejas, the information provided is used only to respond to your specific inquiry. We do not sell or share your contact info.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please email us at tejascopywrites@gmail.com.
