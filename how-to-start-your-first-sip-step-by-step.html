How to Start Your First SIP in India: Step-by-Step Beginner Guide

Written by Tejas Waghmare  •  Published: 20 June 2026  •  Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

Educational guide only, not personalized investment or tax advice. Rules and rates can change; verify official sources and see our disclaimer.

You can start your first Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) in under 20 minutes using PAN, Aadhaar, and an active savings bank account—without needing a Demat account. Choose a Direct-Growth Nifty 50 Index or Flexi-Cap fund on MFCentral or an AMC/broker platform and set a NACH e-Mandate 4–6 days after salary credit.

1. What You Need Ready (15-Minute Document Checklist)

  • PAN Card linked with Aadhaar: Mandatory for mutual fund investments. Confirm PAN-Aadhaar linking status at incometax.gov.in before beginning e-KYC.
  • Aadhaar-linked active mobile number: Needed for DigiLocker / UIDAI OTP verification during CKYC registration.
  • Savings Bank Account (NetBanking or UPI): Used for penny-drop verification, NACH / UPI Autopay e-Mandate registration, and direct redemption credit.
  • Short live video selfie: Taken inside your investment platform or AMC app for In-Person Verification (IPV).

2. Step-by-Step Setup: Platform, Direct Plan & e-Mandate

  1. Complete one-time SEBI CKYC: Enter PAN, authenticate via Aadhaar OTP, verify bank account details, and record the brief live KYC selfie. Verification typically completes within a few hours to 24 hours and works across all AMCs once status becomes "KYC Validated."
  2. Choose a Direct-Plan platform: Invest commission-free through MFCentral (joint registrar platform by CAMS and KFintech holding units in Statement of Account format across AMCs), individual AMC websites, or Direct-only apps (Zerodha Coin, Groww, Kuvera). Ensure the scheme name explicitly reads "Direct Plan — Growth".
  3. Pick your starter fund by time horizon: For 7+ year goals, a single Direct-Growth Nifty 50 Index Fund keeps costs low (typical Direct TER around 0.17%–0.20%) and removes stock-selection risk. For 3–5 year goals, pair debt stability with controlled equity via an Aggressive Hybrid or Short Duration Debt Fund rather than pure small-cap equity.
  4. Set amount, buffer date, and e-Mandate: Most funds allow starting at ₹500/month. Schedule your SIP debit 4–6 days after salary credit (for instance, the 6th or 7th if paid on the 1st) and approve your One-Time Mandate (OTM) / UPI Autopay so installments never bounce over weekend delays.

3. Why Direct Plans Matter: Worked Rupee Comparison

Both Direct and Regular plans hold the exact same portfolio under the same fund manager. The difference is a recurring annual trail commission (commonly ~0.7%–1.0% extra TER on equity Regular plans) deducted daily from Regular NAV. Here is the exact compounding formula FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) / i] × (1 + i) verified for a ₹15,000 monthly SIP over 25 years (300 months, total invested ₹45,00,000):

4. What Happens in Your First 3 Months (Illustrative Example)

Illustrative example, not a real person: Suppose Aditi sets up a ₹3,000/month Direct-Growth Nifty 50 SIP debited on the 7th. In Month 1, ₹3,000 buys units and normal daily market movement may leave her account at ₹2,960 or ₹3,040. By Month 3 (₹9,000 total invested), even at a 12% annualized pace, theoretical gains are under ₹200–₹270 because early installments have been invested for only 30–90 days. Early SIP months build habit and accumulate units; meaningful compounding occurs in years 7–20.

5. Equity Mutual Fund Tax Rules (FY 2026-27)

  • Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG >12 months): Taxed at 12.5% (plus applicable surcharge/cess) on realized gains above the annual ₹1,25,000 exemption limit. Note: each SIP monthly installment has its own separate 12-month FIFO holding clock.
  • Short-Term Capital Gains (STCG ≤12 months): Taxed at 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do I need to start my first SIP in India?

Most SEBI-registered Indian mutual funds accept SIPs starting from ₹500 per month (and a few index schemes allow ₹100). Complete e-KYC once via PAN and Aadhaar before initiating your first debit.

Do I need a Demat account to start a mutual fund SIP?

No. Mutual funds can be held completely free in Statement of Account (SoA) format through MFCentral, AMC portals, or platforms like Kuvera without annual Demat maintenance charges.

What happens if I miss a SIP date due to low bank balance?

The mutual fund AMC does not charge a penalty, nor does missing a SIP affect your credit score, though your bank typically charges ₹250–₹550 + GST for NACH mandate return. See our guide on what happens if you miss a SIP payment for full details.

Should I pause my SIP during a market correction?

If your income and emergency fund remain intact, falling markets allow the same rupee SIP to purchase more mutual fund units at lower NAVs. Pause or reduce only if cash flow emergencies require prioritizing immediate household liquidity.
