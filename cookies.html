Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Cookies Do

Cookies are tiny text files stored in your browser. Nothing fancy gets hidden. Most websites rely on cookies to keep basic toggles working across tabs or tally visitor counts without storing personal bank balances or payslip figures on remote databases.

2. Cookies Set on Calculatorship

Three light categories apply cleanly across the domain:

  • Interface state: Saves lightweight local preferences such as display state and mobile navigation drawer toggles so pages behave cleanly as you move between tools. Logins never apply.
  • Traffic measurement: Google Analytics 4 logs aggregate session patterns, device screen widths, and slow-loading routes across calculators so broken mobile tables get fixed quickly while every rupee slider input remains offline inside local device RAM. Stats stay aggregate.
  • Automated display slots: Google AdSense may read standard browser preferences under Google privacy controls to display clearly separated page ads. Inputs stay invisible.

3. Zero Lead-Gen Selling

Lead-broker pixels stay banned completely. Nobody sells phone numbers, salary brackets, or loan queries to insurance telemarketers or bank aggregators. Privacy comes first.

4. Blocking Cookies Anytime

Disable cookies anytime inside Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. Calculators still work fine. Client-side JavaScript computes SIP growth, tax slabs, and EMI schedules entirely inside local device memory even when every browser cookie stays blocked.
