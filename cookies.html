Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026  |  Effective for all users visiting calculatorship.in

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies Calculatorship uses, and how you can control them. It applies to all pages on calculatorship.in and complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow websites to remember your preferences, measure how you interact with pages, and serve relevant advertisements. Cookies do not contain your name, email address, or payment information unless you have explicitly provided such data.

2. Cookie Categories We Use

2.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the site to function correctly. They cannot be disabled as they are set in response to your actions (e.g., saving your cookie consent decision).

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration
calculatorship_cookie_consent calculatorship.in Stores your cookie consent choice (accepted / declined) 1 year (localStorage)

2.2 Analytics Cookies (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors interact with our calculators and guides. GA4 collects anonymised information such as pages visited, time on site, and device type. No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected by GA4 on this site. Analytics cookies are only loaded after you give consent via our cookie banner.

Cookie Name Provider Purpose Duration

_gid Google (GA4) Identifies users across sessions for daily aggregated statistics 24 hours

Opt out of Google Analytics: You can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or decline cookies via our cookie banner below.

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: ca-pub-7598871729388798) to display relevant advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to show you personalised ads based on your browsing history across the web, using the DoubleClick and Google Ads networks. Advertising cookies are only loaded after you give consent via our cookie banner.

  • Visit Google Ad Settings to manage your ad personalisation preferences
  • Use the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out tool
  • Or simply click Decline in our cookie banner at the bottom of any page on this site

Our site is served via Cloudflare for DDoS protection, CDN acceleration, and Web Analytics. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies and use its own analytics beacon.

3. Managing & Withdrawing Consent

You can control cookies in the following ways:

  • Our cookie banner: When you first visit the site, you can choose Accept All or Decline. Your choice is saved locally and can be cleared by clearing your browser's localStorage for calculatorship.in.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Consult your browser's help documentation:
    • Google Chrome
    • Mozilla Firefox
    • Apple Safari
    • Microsoft Edge
  • Google ad personalisation: Google Ad Settings
  • NAI opt-out: networkadvertising.org

Note: Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect core site functionality (e.g., theme preferences).

4. Do We Use Cross-Site Tracking?

Calculatorship itself does not track you across other websites. However, Google Analytics and Google AdSense cookies may be used by Google to show you personalised content or ads on other Google-affiliated properties, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at contact@calculatorship.in or via our Contact Page.
