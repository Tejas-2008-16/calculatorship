Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files saved by your web browser when you visit a website. They help websites remember basic interface settings, measure aggregate page performance, and serve relevant display ads without storing your personal calculator inputs on our servers.

2. Types of Cookies Used on Calculatorship

Calculatorship uses three straightforward categories of browser storage and cookies:

  • Essential Interface State: Lightweight browser storage remembers navigation drawer states and basic page preferences as you move between calculators and guides. No account login is required.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Aggregate Measurement): We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which calculators and guides readers visit, device screen sizes, and page speed issues. None of the rupee amounts you type into a calculator are ever sent to analytics.
  • Google AdSense (Advertising Cookies): Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to Calculatorship or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

3. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn how Google uses data from partner sites at Google Advertising Privacy & Terms, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies via aboutads.info.

4. Managing or Blocking Cookies in Your Browser

You can clear or block cookies anytime inside Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge settings. Every calculator on Calculatorship runs in client-side JavaScript inside your browser memory, so SIP, EMI, PPF, and Income Tax calculations continue working even if all cookies are blocked.
