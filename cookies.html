Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser when visiting a website. They help websites remember preferences or measure anonymous visitor patterns.

2. Cookies Used on Calculatorship

Calculatorship uses minimal cookies:

  • Essential site preferences: Used by your browser to remember UI state, such as mobile navigation drawer interactions.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): Anonymously measure general traffic metrics (e.g. number of visitors per page). No personal financial numbers or contact details are tracked.

3. No Advertising Cookies

We do not run third-party advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or ad networks on Calculatorship.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can adjust your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge) to block or delete cookies at any time. Calculators on this site will continue to function fully with cookies disabled, because all financial calculations execute entirely in client-side JavaScript.
