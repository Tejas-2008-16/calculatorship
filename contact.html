Report a Calculation Error

Our mathematical models are built to strictly follow AMFI formulas, Reserve Bank of India amortization standards, and Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) schedules. However, edge conditions or recently revised statutory limits (such as new rebate thresholds or quarterly interest adjustments) may occasionally arise.

If you suspect an arithmetic discrepancy on any tool:

  1. Mention the specific tool page URL (e.g. /emi-calculator.html or /ppf-calculator.html).
  2. Provide the exact input variables you entered (e.g. Principal, Tenure, Interest Rate, compounding frequency).
  3. State the generated output versus your expected calculated output, including references to the applicable statutory or actuarial rule.
