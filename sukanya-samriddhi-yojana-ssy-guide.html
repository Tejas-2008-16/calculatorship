1. Key Rules, Eligibility & Account Mechanics

Launched as part of the Government's 'Beti Bachao, Beti Padhao' campaign, the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) stands as India's highest-yielding sovereign debt savings scheme. Designed exclusively for parents of girl children, it combines maximum sovereign safety with exceptional post-tax returns.

Scheme Feature Official Statutory Rule
Target Beneficiary Resident girl child aged between 0 and 10 years
Current Sovereign Interest Rate 8.20% per annum (Compounded annually)
Annual Deposit Limits Min: ₹250 | Max: ₹1,50,000 per financial year
Mandatory Deposit Duration 15 Years from account opening date
Account Maturity Tenure 21 Years from opening (or marriage after 18)
Income Tax Status EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt): Zero tax on deposit, interest & maturity

2. Mathematical Wealth Model: How ₹1.5L Annual Deposit Builds ₹71+ Lakh

Consider a parent opening an SSY account upon the birth of their daughter in 2026 and investing the maximum allowable amount of ₹1,50,000 on the 1st of April every year for 15 consecutive years. Assuming the current 8.20% rate holds across the tenure:

Tenure Milestone Cumulative Deposit Paid Cumulative Interest Accrued Total Account Balance
End of Year 5 ₹7,50,000 ₹1,96,870 ₹9,46,870
End of Year 10 ₹15,00,000 ₹8,82,490 ₹23,82,490
End of Year 15 (Deposits End) ₹22,50,000 ₹23,96,250 ₹46,46,250
End of Year 21 (Final Maturity) ₹22,50,000 ₹49,32,000 ₹71,82,000 (~₹71.82 Lakh)

Over the 21-year horizon, your total capital outlay of ₹22.50 Lakh generates nearly ₹49.32 Lakh in completely tax-free compound interest, producing an inflation-hedged corpus of nearly ₹72 Lakh for your daughter's higher education.

4. Partial Withdrawal Rules for Higher Education

To fund university entrance fees and tuition, SSY incorporates structured partial withdrawal rules:

  • Eligibility: Allowed once the girl child attains 18 years of age or passes the 10th standard.
  • Withdrawal Limit: Up to 50% of the total account balance standing at the end of the preceding financial year.
  • Payment Mode: Can be withdrawn in a single lump sum or in up to 5 equal annual installments matching university fee cycles, supported by verified admission letters.

5. Step-by-Step Account Opening & Operational Checklist

  1. Authorized Outlets: Open an account at any Post Office branch or designated public/private banks (SBI, PNB, Canara, HDFC, ICICI, Axis).
  2. Required Documentation: Child's official Birth Certificate, Parent/Guardian PAN and Aadhaar, and passport-size photographs.
  3. Timing Rule: To maximize compound interest accrual, deposit your annual contribution between April 1st and April 5th of each financial year. Interest is calculated on the minimum balance between the 5th and end of each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the current interest rate for Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) in 2026?

The Government of India offers an attractive, sovereign-guaranteed interest rate of 8.20% per annum on Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana accounts, compounded annually. Interest rates on small savings schemes are reviewed quarterly by the Ministry of Finance.

What is the maximum and minimum deposit limit in an SSY account per financial year?

The minimum annual deposit is ₹250, while the maximum allowable deposit is ₹1,50,000 per financial year per girl child account. Deposits can be made in multiples of ₹50 via cash, cheque, demand draft, or online net banking/UPI.

How long must I deposit money in SSY, and when does the account mature?

Deposits are required for a period of 15 years from the date of account opening. The account reaches full maturity upon completion of 21 years from the opening date, or upon the marriage of the girl child after attaining age 18.

What is the tax status of Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana?

SSY enjoys the prestigious Exempt-Exempt-Exempt (EEE) tax status under the Old Tax Regime. The initial investment qualifies for deduction under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 Lakh), the annual compounded interest is 100% tax-free under Section 10, and the entire maturity payout is completely exempt from income tax.

Can I open SSY accounts for three daughters?

As a general rule, SSY accounts can only be opened for a maximum of two girl children in a family. However, an exception is permitted for a third account if the birth order results in twin or triplet girl children in the first or second delivery, supported by hospital medical certificates.
