Free Financial Calculators for Indian Users

Every calculator on Calculatorship is self-contained, pre-renders a verified default example, cites its mathematical formula, and references the underlying statutory authority.

Educational Notice: These tools provide mathematical projections and illustrations based on historical compound formulas and current Indian tax statutes (FY 2026-27). They are not financial recommendations. Consult a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser or a Chartered Accountant for personal decision-making.

1. Investing & Wealth Compounding

SIP Calculator

Project monthly mutual fund Systematic Investment Plan returns, capital gains tax liability, and compounding timelines.

Step-Up SIP Calculator

Model annual SIP increases (5% to 15%) in line with salary increments to observe accelerated wealth accumulation.

Lumpsum Calculator

Calculate long-term compounding growth on one-time investments across different equity and debt CAGR expectations.

CAGR Calculator

Compute Compound Annual Growth Rates between initial and final values, or perform reverse CAGR projections.

Compound Interest Calculator

Universal compound interest calculator with daily, monthly, quarterly, and annual compounding frequencies.

Simple Interest Calculator

Calculate non-compounded interest accrual on loans, fixed notes, and short-term capital arrangements.

2. Retirement & Government Savings Schemes

PPF Calculator

Calculate 15-30 year Public Provident Fund maturity with the verified 7.1% interest rate and the 5th-of-the-month rule.

EPF Calculator

Simulate Employee Provident Fund growth at 8.25% p.a., breaking down employee and employer contributions and EPS splits.

NPS Calculator

National Pension System retirement corpus planner with lump-sum withdrawal vs monthly annuity pension options.

SWP Calculator

Plan regular monthly retirement cash flows from your mutual fund corpus while calculating portfolio longevity.

FD Calculator

Calculate Fixed Deposit maturity for cumulative and periodic interest payouts across Indian bank rates.

Recurring Deposit (RD) Calculator

Bank RD maturity calculator using the quarterly compounding formula prescribed by Indian banking regulations.

3. Taxation, Salary & Statutory Benefits

Income Tax Calculator

Compare New vs Old Tax Regime for FY 2026-27 with standard deduction, Section 87A rebate, and marginal relief.

Gratuity Calculator

Calculate retirement and separation gratuity using the Payment of Gratuity Act 15/26 formula and ₹20 Lakh tax exemption limit.

GST Calculator

Compute GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive pricing with automatic CGST, SGST, and IGST breakdowns across official slabs.

HRA Exemption Calculator

Determine your tax-exempt House Rent Allowance under Section 10(13A) using the 3 statutory limits for metro and non-metro cities.

In-Hand Salary Calculator

Convert Cost-to-Company (CTC) into accurate monthly take-home pay after deducting EPF, gratuity provisions, PT, and income tax.

4. Loans, Budgeting & Inflation Planning

Loan EMI & Prepayment Calculator

Calculate home loan, car loan, and personal loan EMIs with full monthly amortization tables and prepayment interest savings.

Target Goal Planner

Work backward from future target sums (higher education, home purchase, retirement) to find the required monthly SIP contribution.

50/30/20 Budget Planner

Organize your monthly salary into Essential Needs (50%), Lifestyle Wants (30%), and Investments/Savings (20%).

Inflation Calculator

Calculate future purchasing power erosion and determine the real value of capital over 5 to 30 years.
