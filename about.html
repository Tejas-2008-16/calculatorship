Our Mission

Calculatorship exists to democratize financial literacy and mathematical clarity for India's 80+ crore working population. In India today, the vast majority of retail investors and salaried individuals lack access to unbiased, fee-only financial planners. As a result, millions of hardworking professionals end up buying opaque insurance-cum-investment policies, miscalculating compound interest, or making critical tax errors due to confusing regulatory jargon.

We believe that high-grade financial math should be completely free, open, and transparent. Calculatorship provides mathematical models, compound growth simulations, and tax optimization algorithms that run instantly without paywalls, email gates, or sales pressure. Our goal is to empower every Indian earner with the exact numbers they need to make confident, long-term wealth decisions.

Editorial Standards & Fact-Checking Process

We adhere to strict editorial integrity and rigorous mathematical verification across all calculator algorithms and articles. In alignment with Google's Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T) principles, our process guarantees:

  • Actuarial Verification: Every calculator compounding formula (e.g. SIP future value with annuity dues, quarterly compounded Fixed Deposits, diminishing balance amortizations for EMIs) is cross-verified against official AMFI calculation standards and standard financial mathematics.
  • Statutory Tax Alignment: All income tax calculations, capital gains tax rules, Section 87A rebate ceilings, and standard deduction figures are updated in exact accordance with the relevant Finance Act passed by the Parliament of India and notifications from the CBDT.
  • Monetary & Small Savings Revisions: Small savings rates (PPF, SSY, SCSS) and bank deposit insurance metrics are reviewed and refreshed whenever the Ministry of Finance or the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) issues quarterly interest rate notifications or repo rate modifications.
  • Objective Independence: Calculatorship does not publish sponsored product placements or prioritize specific mutual fund schemes or banks over others. We do not participate in affiliate commissions that influence calculation outcomes.
  • Mandatory Advisory Referral: Because calculations are mathematical simulations based on user-supplied assumptions, we consistently remind readers to consult a certified SEBI-RIA or Chartered Accountant before executing capital allocations or filing tax returns.

Indian Regulatory Oversight & Investor Grievance Redressal

Indian retail investors and consumers are protected by statutory grievance redressal mechanisms established by India's primary financial regulators: the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI), the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), and the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA).

Regulator / Segment Primary Grievance Portal Mandated Resolution SLA
SEBI (Mutual Funds, Stocks, Demat) SEBI SCORES 2.0 (scores.sebi.gov.in) / SMART ODR 21 Calendar Days
RBI (Banks, NBFCs, UPI, Loans) RBI Integrated Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in) 30 Calendar Days
IRDAI (Life & Health Insurance) Bima Bharosa Portal (bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in) 15 Calendar Days
PFRDA (NPS & APY) Central Grievance Management System (CGMS) 30 Calendar Days

If an entity fails to resolve your grievance within 30 days or provides an unsatisfactory resolution, you can escalate directly to the respective Ombudsman or Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal without incurring any legal fees.
