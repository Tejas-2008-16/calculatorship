About Calculatorship

Calculatorship is built and maintained by Tejas, a self-taught personal finance enthusiast based in India. Tejas is not a SEBI-registered investment advisor, Chartered Accountant, or licensed financial planner. He built Calculatorship to make financial math accessible to everyday Indian investors through free tools and plain-English guides.

All content is independently researched using publicly available regulatory data from SEBI, AMFI, RBI, and the Income Tax Department of India. AI writing tools are used to help organize and structure content. Nothing on this platform constitutes professional financial advice.

Our Core Principles

  • 100% Free & Independent: We are not an Asset Management Company (AMC) distributor and do not sell financial products. Our calculations remain completely objective.
  • Transparent Authorship: Tejas is a personal finance enthusiast, not a certified financial advisor. All content is written for general educational awareness only.
  • Data Privacy by Design: None of your financial figures, salary inputs, or calculation variables are stored on our servers. All simulations execute locally in your web browser.
  • Updated for Indian Regulations: Our tax calculators and compounding formulas are updated in accordance with the latest Finance Acts and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) notifications.

Who Is This For?

Calculatorship is designed for Indian salaried professionals, first-time investors, retirees, and anyone who wants to understand personal finance better through free tools and plain-English guides — without expensive advisors or complicated jargon.
