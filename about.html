Why This Site Exists

Many online financial calculators require a phone number or email before showing results, or push you to sign up for loans or specific investment products. I wanted a clean place where anyone can enter an amount, adjust an interest rate or duration slider, and see how compounding, loans, and taxes work without pop-ups or product pitches.

How Guides & Numbers Are Verified

I draft guides by reading public circulars and official publications from government portals like the Income Tax Department (incometaxindia.gov.in), SEBI (sebi.gov.in), AMFI (amfiindia.com), and the RBI (rbi.org.in). I write out the explanations, use AI tools to assist with drafting and structuring the text, and then check each number, formula, and limit by hand against the official source before publishing. Every guide carries a note stating when its figures were last reviewed.

Read our full Editorial & Verification Policy and view our public Corrections Log.
