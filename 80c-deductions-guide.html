Section 80C Deductions in India: Eligible Options, Limits & Rules

Written by Tejas Waghmare  •  Published: 07 July 2026  •  Last reviewed: 23 November 2026  •  Tax Law: Sections 80C & 80CCE (Old Tax Regime)

Educational tax guide only. Not personal chartered accountancy or tax advice. Section 80C deductions apply exclusively under the Old Tax Regime and are unavailable under the default New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC). Read our full disclaimer.

Section 80C of the Income-tax Act allows individual taxpayers and HUFs to deduct up to ₹1,50,000 per financial year from their taxable income by investing in eligible instruments like EPF, PPF, ELSS, and Life Insurance, or paying children tuition fees and home loan principal. Note that Section 80C is available exclusively under the Old Tax Regime; the default New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) disallows all 80C deductions.
